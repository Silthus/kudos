# Stars

Stars: a light thank-you by reaction (SR1–SR10, SR12–SR16, SR20, SR21, SR24), never an award. The Slack adapter hands it a normalized reaction, the workspace, the giver and the message's author as chat user IDs, the conversation, the message's ts and the reaction's time, and nothing else of the event; giveStar decides it in the mutation that records the event's receipt: a reaction 24 hours or more after the message, or before it, does nothing; a giver or author the directory doesn't know answers unknown people for the adapter to look up, and the reaction is decided again as at its time once they are known; a giver or author who isn't a member does nothing; on a Given kudos message, read through the engine's exported reads (its author and the giver's own award on it, unique lookups), the star is for its recipients, and the author or a recipient starring it does nothing; on any other message it is for the author, never the giver. A giver stars a message once, and gives at most their star budget of stars on their day (R12), 10 unless the workspace row sets starBudget, which a maintainer sets with an internal mutation; the once-check, the budget count and the insert-only star record are read and written in that one mutation, so a star past the budget writes nothing and the same member can star that message on a later day within its 24 hours. Only a reaction that passes every other check reads the giver's `stars` member decision, with exposure, and does nothing while it is off. A recorded star pays the giver 1 XP through progression's recordReward, keyed `star-given:{conversation}:{ts}`, and publishes starGiven; payStarPeople, its subscriber, then pays each of its people 1 XP keyed `star:{giver}:{conversation}:{ts}`, 100 recipients a mutation from the engine's paged read of the message's awards and continuing from its cursor, skipping a recipient who is no longer a member, and writes one row per star and person, so a rerun pays nobody twice. Reward records carry no cause, no coins and no item, at catalogue version `stars-1`; the people are paid whatever their own flag says. No message, ephemeral, DM or reaction is sent for a star, and nothing that reads awards sees one. The star's writes keep its counts (SR14): the star's mutation adds it, with its XP, to the giver's row for their week (Monday to Sunday of their day) and to the message's all-time star count; payStarPeople adds it, with its XP, to each person's row for their own week at the star's time, and to that person's count for the message that week with the time of its last star, from which the row keeps the week's most-starred message, the latest last star winning a tie. A week's row starts waiting for its recap (SR12). An hourly sweep reads only waiting rows and stranded ones, 100 a mutation, again while a page is full: it schedules a waiting row's recap at Monday 09:00 after its week in the member's time zone, as the Monday post schedules its wall-clock time, and starts again a scheduled recap whose try Convex stopped (more than Convex's action time limit past when it was due), at most 3 tries in all. Each try reads the row first and goes ahead only for the try the row awaits, so a redelivered sweep, a second sweep in the same hour or a stale job sends nothing more, and waits again for Monday 09:00 when the member's time zone now puts it later; the recap is skipped for someone no longer a member, for whom `xp-levels` is off, whose `stars-recap` place isn't `dm`, or, read last with exposure, for whom `stars` is off. Otherwise the try records its start and hands the Slack adapter SR12's copy, with the workspace's kudos emoji, a zero count left out, `1 star` singular and the most-starred line only when they received stars and the workspace URL is known, linked by its permalink, and the message settings footer while `message-settings` is on. A recap Slack took is marked sent and reported as stars recap sent with only the stars given and received and their XP (SR16); one Slack refused is tried again as a level-up DM is, and is marked failed after its third try.

## invariants
- a member stars a message at most once: A giver's reactions on one message make one star, which pays its author or recipients and the giver 1 XP once in all and reaches PostHog as one star given, whether the event is redelivered, the reaction is removed and added again or given again with another skin tone.
  over: a redelivered event, a removed and re-added reaction and a second skin tone on an ordinary message, and a redelivered star on a Given kudos message with two recipients
  via: SR5 SR23: a member stars a message at most once
  refuted: giveStar no longer looked for the giver's star on the message -> a re-added reaction and a second skin tone each made a second star and a second star given (2026-10-16)
  because: a star is a one-click thank-you worth 1 XP each way; paying it again for every redelivery, re-add or skin tone would let a member farm XP for themselves and a teammate from one message (SR5)
  kinds: state
  checklist: revalidated-permission dismissed: no permission is involved; the star's mutation reads the giver's membership and the message's awards when it decides
  checklist: separation-of-duties dismissed: no approval policy applies to a star
  checklist: legal-state-succession declared as a member stars a message at most once
  checklist: supersession-safety dismissed: a star record is insert-only, unique per message and giver, and a later reaction finds it in its own transaction
  checklist: worker-fencing dismissed: no worker owns a star; the decision is one mutation and the people's pages are keyed per star and person
  checklist: commit-ordered-effects dismissed: a star sends nothing to Slack; its XP and starGiven commit with the record
  checklist: resumption-coverage dismissed: payStarPeople continues from the recipients' cursor, and a page rerun skips each person whose row exists and each reward whose key is recorded
- a star never passes the star budget: A giver's stars on their day never pay past their star budget, 10 unless the workspace sets another, even when two reactions arrive at once for the last star left.
  over: eleven stars on one day, the twelfth the next day, a workspace budget of 2, and two reactions at once after nine stars
  via: SR6 SR23: a star never passes the star budget
  refuted: giveStar refused a star only when the stars left went below 0 instead of at 0 -> 3 of 3 tests failed: the giver got 11 XP from 11 stars, both concurrent reactions paid past the 10th, and a budget of 2 paid 3 (2026-10-16)
  because: the budget keeps stars light (SR6); a check and a write in separate transactions would let two reactions at once both take the tenth star
  kinds: state
  checklist: revalidated-permission dismissed: no permission is involved; the budget is counted in the mutation that records the star
  checklist: separation-of-duties dismissed: no approval policy applies to a star
  checklist: legal-state-succession declared as a star never passes the star budget
  checklist: supersession-safety dismissed: Convex serializes two mutations that read the same giver's stars that day, so the second sees the first's record or retries
  checklist: worker-fencing dismissed: no worker holds the budget; each reaction's mutation counts and records on its own
  checklist: commit-ordered-effects dismissed: a star past the budget writes and sends nothing; a star within it commits its record, XP and starGiven together
  checklist: resumption-coverage dismissed: no batch counts the budget; a looked-up star is decided again in one mutation as at its time
- a star is never an award: A star changes no award, board, kudos total, allowance, coin, quest, Journey step, sticker or gate decision; it writes only its own records, reward records and XP, and its flag's exposure.
  over: three stars, on a Given kudos message and on ordinary messages, by members who each gave and got kudos, with every feature on, compared table by table with the state before them
  via: SR2 SR23: a star is never an award
  refuted: recordStar paid the giver's star reward 1 coin instead of none -> three givers' wallets each read 1 coin above the state before the stars (2026-10-16)
  because: stars sit below kudos on purpose (SR2): a star that reached awards would count on boards and kudos totals, spend or refill no allowance yet earn coins, finish quests and Journey steps, or reach the meaningful-thank-you classifier
  kinds: state
  checklist: revalidated-permission dismissed: no permission is involved; a star reads awards only to find its people
  checklist: separation-of-duties dismissed: no approval policy applies to a star
  checklist: legal-state-succession declared as a star is never an award
  checklist: supersession-safety dismissed: a star writes no award-side row, so no award-side state can be displaced by it
  checklist: worker-fencing dismissed: no worker owns a star's writes
  checklist: commit-ordered-effects dismissed: starGiven reaches only payStarPeople and analytics, never a module that reads awards
  checklist: resumption-coverage dismissed: payStarPeople pays only reward records keyed per star and person, whatever page it resumes from
