# Achievements

Achievements: owns each member's ladders and the tiers they earn, and the Book that shows them (P58 to P61, P75, P76, P78, P79, P93). The ladder catalogue is versioned code with a stable ID for every ladder, tier and tier reward; the names are copy. It holds the two ladders that need nothing unshipped: Generosity, counting kudos given, and Recognition, counting kudos received, each with bronze at 1, silver at 25, gold at 100 and diamond at 500, and each tier names its promised reward by kind and reward ID: bronze coins, silver a kudos emoji, gold a hedgehog, diamond coins and a hedgehog. The Book ships with the achievements flag in Unlocks' schedule and opens achievements, so it becomes ready at level 4 only where the achievements flag is on, and its readiness copy names nothing that hasn't shipped. The Book applies in a workspace only where the achievements flag (a workspace flag, off by default), xp-levels are on and unlocks applies there; every mutation and query that shows the Book reads that one predicate. The first mutation that sees the Book apply in a workspace stores the Book's launch once, with its time: Achievements' kudosGiven subscriber, App Home's publish, or any of Unlocks' mutations that sees unlocks live there (its own launch, readiness on a level up, the ship sweep, an opening or a command's reply), so the Book is never ready before it is launched. Nothing is counted before the launch, and the launch counts the workspace's whole history (P62): the first mutation that sees a ladder or tier ID the workspace hasn't evaluated, the launch itself or the first one after a deploy that adds one, records those IDs as evaluated and schedules their replay, so each ladder and tier is evaluated once per workspace. A ladder new to the workspace is replayed over every Given award of the workspace in award order, 100 awards per mutation, each mutation scheduling the next; a tier added to a ladder is evaluated from the count stored with each counted award, once that ladder's history is counted (a tier added while it is still being counted waits for it), so nothing is counted again and the tiers already earned stay as they are; each replay earns only the tiers it records. The replay and the kudosGiven subscriber share the award-keyed counting, so an award counts once whichever of them reaches it first: the subscriber counts a ladder only once that ladder's replay has finished, and the replay reads to the last award, so it catches up every award given while it ran, in award order. All the ladders and tiers one mutation finds unevaluated form one launch batch: every tier a replay earns carries its batch, in its record and in achievementEarned, and the batch is complete only when all its replays are; nobody is told about a batch yet. From the launch on the subscriber counts every kudos, whatever the flags say later, so turning a flag off and on again loses nothing. Each award counts once per member and ladder, keyed by the award, in kudos units, whatever its gate decision (P13): toward the giver's Generosity and the recipient's Recognition, so a redelivered kudosGiven counts nothing twice, and the count after it is stored with it. When an award takes a count from below a tier's count to at or above it, the tier is earned: it is stored once per member, ladder and tier, with the time of the award that reached it, that award, the catalogue version and the promised rewards, marked hidden when the subscriber earns it while the Book doesn't apply, so it is never announced, not even in a later summary, and never changed or taken back, so a kudos made earlier but counted later never moves a tier's date; Achievements publishes achievementEarned for it, keyed by member, ladder and tier, in the same mutation. No reward is granted yet: the reward's kind grants it when that kind ships. While the Book is shown, the subscriber hands the tiers a kudos earns each member to tellTiersEarned in the same mutation, which tells members who had opened the Book by the kudos' time; a tier earned while the Book doesn't apply is never handed over. After a kudos counts for a member who has opened the Book, while it applies, Achievements republishes their App Home. App Home gets a Book tab once the member has opened the Book while it applies, showing each ladder's count, every earned tier with its name and date in the member's time zone, and the next tier with the count it needs, never whom to thank. The website reads achievementsOf, whose shape stays stable: each ladder in catalogue order with its ID, name, what it counts and its count, every tier in ladder order with its ID, name, threshold and promised rewards, and, once earned, its earned time and catalogue version, and the next tier's ID, and nothing where the Book doesn't apply or hasn't launched.
