# Achievements

Achievements: owns each member's ladders and the tiers they earn, and the Book that shows them (P58 to P61, P75, P76, P78, P79, P93). The ladder catalogue is versioned code with a stable ID for every ladder, tier and tier reward; the names are copy. It holds the two ladders that need nothing unshipped: Generosity, counting kudos given, and Recognition, counting kudos received, each with bronze at 1, silver at 25, gold at 100 and diamond at 500, and each tier names its promised reward by kind and reward ID: bronze coins, silver a kudos emoji, gold a hedgehog, diamond coins and a hedgehog. The Book ships with the achievements flag in Unlocks' schedule and opens achievements, so it becomes ready at level 4 only where the achievements flag is on, and its readiness copy names nothing that hasn't shipped. The Book applies in a workspace only where the achievements flag (a workspace flag, off by default), xp-levels are on and unlocks applies there; every mutation and query that shows the Book reads that one predicate. The first mutation that sees the Book apply in a workspace stores the Book's launch once, with its time: Achievements' kudosGiven subscriber, App Home's publish, or any of Unlocks' mutations that sees unlocks live there (its own launch, readiness on a level up, the ship sweep, an opening or a command's reply), so the Book is never ready before it is launched. Nothing is counted before the launch: the kudosGiven subscriber counts only a kudos whose time is at or after it, and leaves what came before to the replay. From the launch on it counts every kudos, whatever the flags say later, so turning a flag off and on again loses nothing. Each award counts once per member and ladder, keyed by the award, in kudos units, whatever its gate decision (P13): toward the giver's Generosity and the recipient's Recognition, so a redelivered kudosGiven counts nothing twice. When a count reaches a tier's count, the tier is earned: it is stored once per member, ladder and tier, with the time of the award that reached it, that award, the catalogue version and the promised rewards, and marked hidden when the Book didn't apply as it was earned, so it is never announced, not even in a later summary, and never changed or taken back, so a kudos made earlier but counted later never moves a tier's date; Achievements publishes achievementEarned for it, keyed by member, ladder and tier, in the same mutation. No reward is granted yet: the reward's kind grants it when that kind ships. While the Book is shown, the same mutation hands the tiers a kudos earns each member to tellTiersEarned, which tells members who have opened the Book; a tier earned while the Book doesn't apply is never handed over, so it is never announced. After a kudos counts for a member who has opened the Book, while it applies, Achievements republishes their App Home. App Home gets a Book tab once the member has opened the Book while it applies, showing each ladder's count, every earned tier with its name and date in the member's time zone, and the next tier with the count it needs, never whom to thank. The website reads achievementsOf, whose shape stays stable: each ladder in catalogue order with its ID, name, what it counts and its count, every tier in ladder order with its ID, name, threshold and promised rewards, and, once earned, its earned time and catalogue version, and the next tier's ID, and nothing where the Book doesn't apply or hasn't launched.
