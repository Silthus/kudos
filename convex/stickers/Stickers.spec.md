# Stickers

Stickers: the people a member collects in the Book (SB1–SB6, decision 27): one sticker of each other member they gave a kudos award to or received one from, kept forever, also after that person leaves. It owns the insert-only sticker records `{ member, person, firstAward, firstAt }`, unique per member and person, where `firstAward` is the pair's earliest award in award order in either direction and `firstAt` its time; the `sticker-book` flag, a member decision off by default, read without exposure by `stickersShownTo`; the kudosGiven subscriber `recordStickers`, which records the stickers of at most 50 of a message's awards per mutation, each mutation scheduling the next, and starts the workspace's backfill when it has none; and the backfill, `startStickerBackfill` and its page `backfillStickers`, which pages one workspace's awards in award order, 50 per mutation, keeping its cursor in the workspace's backfill record and reading to the last award. Both writers go through `recordStickersOf`, which reads whether each of the pair's two stickers exists, and, for a missing one, the pair's earliest award in each direction through the engine's `earliestAwardFrom`, recording the earlier, so either can run first and run again. Stickers and the backfill are recorded whatever the flag says; nothing is told to anyone when a sticker is recorded. `stickersNewestFirst` answers one member's stickers, newest `firstAt` first, which the website's `getCollection` shows while `sticker-book` is on for the member, with each person's name and face as the directory has them now. `albumsOf` derives the member's group albums when they are read and stores nothing: one for every counting group Groups' countingGroupsWithPeopleIn answers, its people the group's members other than the owner, each held when the owner has their sticker, counted as held of total, the owner's own groups first and then by name; the website's getCollection shows them while `sticker-book` is on for the member and groups are shown to them. `teamPagesOf` derives the member's team pages when they are read and stores nothing (CV22–CV25): one for every company team `companyTeamsOf` answers, its people the team's profiles that the matcher's `companyProfilesIn` gives to a current member other than the owner, in posthog.com's order, each held when the owner has their sticker, counted as held of total, its `unmatched` the team's profiles given to no member, mine when the owner's own profile is on the team; a crest the owner collected by its roster stays on the team page of its slug, or on a page with no people that isn't listed where posthog.com has no such team; the owner's own teams first and then by name. The website's getCollection shows them in place of the albums and crests while `team-pages` and `sticker-book` are on for the member. Completed team pages (CV31–CV34, CV36, CV37) ship behind `team-spirit`, a member decision off by default: when `recordStickers` records a new sticker for a member it schedules one `checkTeamPages` for that member, keyed by the award that gave it, once per member per mutation, and the backfill schedules none. The check does nothing unless `sticker-book` and `team-pages` show to the member, then decides `team-spirit` with exposure through `teamSpiritAppliesTo` and does nothing where it is off; it reads the member's team pages by `teamPagesOf` and records each listed page that has people and holds all of them and has no completion yet as an insert-only completion `{ member, team, name, crestImage?, award, at, people, told }`, unique per member and team, with the team's name and crest image then, the page's `total` as `people`, and as `award` and `at` the completing award: the `firstAward` and `firstAt` of the page's sticker with the latest `firstAt`, then the latest in award order, so a check that runs late still credits the kudos that filled the page. It records the first 25 by team name per mutation and schedules itself again while more are due, so the member's completions are its cursor. A member's first check stores the member's `teamPageChecks` record in its first mutation and marks it finished in the mutation that leaves nothing due; every completion recorded before then is `told: false`, and every later one is `told: true` and is told privately under its completing award's message as a completion line, `Team complete · {Team} crest earned`, or `Team complete · {Team}` where the member's roster already held the crest, riding the tier notification of that message with the Team Spirit tier it reached, `team-spirit` and the member's message setting for tiers checked at delivery, and never a public line. Each check mutation that records completions publishes one `teamPagesCompleted` per completing award, which analytics projects as one `team page completed` per team with the public `team` slug, `people` and `told`, never names. While `team-spirit`, `team-pages` and `sticker-book` are all on for the member, read without exposure by `teamSpiritShownTo`, getCollection's team pages gain `completion` from the record, with its `seen` key `page:{team}:{at}`, `complete` reads the record or a full page, the page's `crest` is the earlier of the completion, keyed `team:{team}:{at}`, and the roster's crest, a recorded page whose team left posthog.com stays as a page with no people that isn't listed, with the record's name and art, and App Home's crest count counts each slug held by roster or by page once. Only this module writes sticker, backfill, completion and check records.
