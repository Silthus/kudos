# Stickers

Stickers: the people a member collects in the Book (SB1–SB6, decision 27): one sticker of each other member they gave a kudos award to or received one from, kept forever, also after that person leaves. It owns the insert-only sticker records `{ member, person, firstAward, firstAt }`, unique per member and person, where `firstAward` is the pair's earliest award in award order in either direction and `firstAt` its time; the `sticker-book` flag, a member decision off by default, read without exposure by `stickersShownTo`; the kudosGiven subscriber `recordStickers`, which records the stickers of at most 50 of a message's awards per mutation, each mutation scheduling the next, and starts the workspace's backfill when it has none; and the backfill, `startStickerBackfill` and its page `backfillStickers`, which pages one workspace's awards in award order, 50 per mutation, keeping its cursor in the workspace's backfill record and reading to the last award. Both writers go through `recordStickersOf`, which reads whether each of the pair's two stickers exists, and, for a missing one, the pair's earliest award in each direction through the engine's `earliestAwardFrom`, recording the earlier, so either can run first and run again. Stickers and the backfill are recorded whatever the flag says; nothing is told to anyone when a sticker is recorded. `stickersNewestFirst` answers one member's stickers, newest `firstAt` first, which the website's `getCollection` shows while `sticker-book` is on for the member, with each person's name and face as the directory has them now. `albumsOf` derives the member's group albums when they are read and stores nothing: one for every counting group Groups' countingGroupsWithPeopleIn answers, its people the group's members other than the owner, each held when the owner has their sticker, counted as held of total, the owner's own groups first and then by name; the website's getCollection shows them while `sticker-book` is on for the member and groups are shown to them. Only this module writes sticker and backfill records.
