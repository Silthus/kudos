# Engine

Kudos engine: observes message events and their deletions, keeps the kudos text of a message while its newest revision is a kudos message (its words as segments, its channel's visibility, its author and that revision's gate decision), replacing it with each newer revision and leaving a word-free tombstone when the message is edited away or deleted in Slack, after which a deletion never lets words be stored again; it answers a message's words as removed at once while the directory no longer knows their author as a member, leaves a removed tombstone when a newer revision or a directory check finds the author is no longer a member, and, on memberLeft or a maintainer's request for one member or a whole workspace, replaces every stored words row of that member or workspace with a removed tombstone in pages of 10, which Convex's 1 MiB document limit keeps within one transaction's reads, that reschedule themselves with only IDs in their arguments and report a failure with a fixed message, after which no later revision stores that message's words again, while words that mention the member stay and later kudos are stored as before; memberLeft and a maintainer's request also record a word-free deletion request for that member or workspace, which keeps the backfill from ever reading or storing the words of a kudos message they wrote, even after the member comes back, while live capture stores their later kudos as before, hydrates the words of a Given message that has no kudos text from a revision the backfill read from Slack, with unknown visibility, only while no kudos text or tombstone exists for it, no deletion request covers its author or its workspace, that revision is at least the newest the engine saw, its author is still a member and it still parses as a kudos message, taking a newer revision it read as the newest the engine saw whether or not it stores words, so an older revision arriving later changes nothing, and never evaluating, awarding or replying, keeps the spotlight current: after every observation, recheck and hydration it gives a message a random position in [0, 1) while it is drawable (Given, its kudos text from a public channel with newest words that said why, its giver still a member, and neither its giver nor any recipient off the leaderboards) and takes the position away otherwise, checking a message with more than 50 people on it in a job of its own with only its ref, as it does whenever the message's words become a tombstone, recomputes every message a member gave or received when that member's leaderboard setting changes, newest first, at most 10 messages or 1,000 people checked a job before the next job resumes after the last one, and walks one workspace's positions for the activity module once around from a seed or from where a walk began, checking each message again and answering skipped once it turned away 25 or once the next message's check and answer would pass the read's 3,000 lookups, checks the directory and rechecks a message that waits on it 1, 6, 36, 156 and 516 minutes after the first miss before abandoning it, decides each attempt's outcome against the daily allowance, writes awards and publishes kudosGiven for a Given attempt and attemptFailed for an Over allowance or Invalid one, reads back every Given message with its awards in award order for the backfills of later subscribers, tells when a giver last thanked each award's recipient before that award, in the order awards were recorded, and answers what App Home shows through homeFacts, a platform-neutral read of a member's facts: the daily allowance left of its base, when it refills (the member's next local midnight, or midnight UTC without a time zone), the kudos emoji, and the kudos given and received this week and in total. homeOf serves those facts to the Slack adapter; any other surface reads the same facts through homeFacts.

## invariants
- only kudos messages' text is stored: The words of a message are stored only while its newest revision is a kudos message, and only in the kudos text: never in another table or a scheduled job's arguments, and never in a captured exception, the analytics outbox or a console line when the events mutation, the directory recheck or hydrate fails.
  over: an ordinary chat message, a kudos message, a kudos message edited away or deleted in Slack, a kudos message whose author leaves Slack, a kudos message the backfill fills, a kudos message the backfill reaches after its author's or its workspace's deletion request, a kudos message the backfill reaches after its author left and came back, and a failure of the events mutation, of the directory recheck or of hydrate with capture on
  via: AT40: only kudos messages' text is stored
  because: the owner's decision of 2026-10-14 (d-9e3c1a90) is that the app keeps the words of kudos messages and nothing else (AT1, amending R51); ordinary chat must never sit in the app's database, and a kudos message's words must stay in one table so deleting them in Slack deletes them everywhere
  refuted: forgetDeleted wrote its deleted tombstone only for a message with no kudos text yet -> the deleting it test found the words still stored (2026-10-14)
  refuted: hydrateWords lost withholdingWords and backfillWords its carriesWords observation -> both thrown in hydrate cases found the words in a captured exception and the rethrown error (2026-10-14)
  refuted: deletionRequested answered false, so neither the backfill's page nor hydrate saw a deletion request -> both deletion request before the backfill cases found the words stored (2026-10-14)
  refuted: forgetWordsOfLeaver recorded no deletion request -> the leaving and coming back before the backfill case found the words stored (2026-10-14)
  kinds: storage
  checklist: scoped-reads dismissed: the invariant is about where words are written; the member reads that confine who reads them are the activity read's (AT14, AT28)
  checklist: encrypted-storage dismissed: the words are kept readable for the member reads by the owner's decision (AT1); no field is designated encrypted at rest
  checklist: key-rotation-compatibility dismissed: nothing is encrypted
  checklist: input-validation dismissed: the segments reach the engine through messageEventValidator at the events mutation's boundary, and through fetchedMessageValidator at hydrate's
  checklist: revision-preservation dismissed: only the newest revision's words are kept (AT4); no earlier revision is kept or addressed
  checklist: commit-ordered-effects dismissed: the kudos text is written in the mutation that records the revision it follows and issues no external effect
  checklist: durable-dispatch-intent dismissed: nothing is dispatched from the kudos text
  checklist: declared-target-coverage dismissed: no fan-out to a registry of targets
  checklist: completion-evidence dismissed: the kudos text is complete when the events mutation commits; nothing is queued
- awards are insert-only: An award, once written, is never changed or deleted, whatever later happens to its message, its giver's allowance or its delivery.
  over: every module under convex/, outside its tests and the generated code
  via: R18: awards are only ever inserted, never changed or deleted
  because: an award is permanent recognition (ADR-0001); an edit, a deletion, a retried delivery or a later module must never take kudos back or change how many were given
  refuted: added withdrawAward to the awards module, which deletes an award by id -> the test failed on its no patch, replace or delete assertion (2026-10-13)
  kinds: storage
  checklist: scoped-reads dismissed: the invariant is about writes; awards are read whole by their recipient, with no shared population restricted by scope
  checklist: encrypted-storage dismissed: an award holds member ids, counts and dates, nothing designated confidential at rest
  checklist: key-rotation-compatibility dismissed: nothing is encrypted
  checklist: input-validation dismissed: the award row is written by the engine from validated rows, never from outside input
  checklist: revision-preservation dismissed: awards have no revisions; there is only the first and only write
  checklist: commit-ordered-effects dismissed: writing an award issues no external effect; the indicator delivery is scheduled in the same transaction and runs only after it commits
  checklist: durable-dispatch-intent dismissed: no external dispatch is tied to the award row itself
  checklist: declared-target-coverage dismissed: no fan-out to a registry of targets; spec 1 registers no subscriber
  checklist: completion-evidence dismissed: an award is complete when its transaction commits; nothing is queued before its effect
- a message gives kudos at most once: A kudos message gives kudos at most once, however often Slack redelivers it and however often its giver edits it, an edit away from a kudos message and back into one included.
  over: a kudos message the engine observes twice, a retry Slack delivers twice, and a message that gave kudos and is then edited away from a kudos message and back into one
  via: R19: a message gives at most once
  because: an award is permanent (ADR-0001), so a second evaluation of a message that already gave would pay its recipients twice and spend the giver's allowance twice; Slack redelivers events and an edit can turn a message back into a kudos message (R19, R26)
  refuted: observeEdit no longer treated a message that gave kudos as accepted -> the edit away and back test gave Bob a second kudos (2026-10-13)
  kinds: message, revision
  checklist: destination-confinement dismissed: the engine sends nothing anywhere; its feedback is delivered by the Slack adapter to one configured base URL
  checklist: message-authenticity dismissed: the engine trusts its caller; the Slack adapter's signed Slack requests only verifies every event before it reaches observe
  checklist: input-validation dismissed: observe takes a MessageEvent that Convex validates against messageEventValidator at the internal mutation's boundary
  checklist: revision-preservation dismissed: a kudos message keeps only its newest revision as a watermark; no history of revisions is published or addressed
  checklist: retry-recognition dismissed: a redelivered event or a repeated edit is recognized by its revision at or below the newest seen and answered as unchanged or stale, never as a conflict
  checklist: duplicate-suppression declared as a message gives kudos at most once
  checklist: keyed-ordering dismissed: no transport ordering is relied on; the newest revision wins whatever order Slack delivers in (R21), and Convex serializes the mutations that touch one message's row
  checklist: acknowledgment-barrier dismissed: the event is acknowledged after the mutation that records its receipt and evaluates it commits
  checklist: retry-classification dismissed: observe calls no external system; delivery retries belong to R35's slice
