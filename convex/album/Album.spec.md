# Album

Album: the collection-first core loop of the album spec (AL1–AL73, `spec/album.md` on `docs/album`), whose slices so far are thoughtful packs (AL1–AL7), opening them into the tray (AL8–AL10, and AL12's drop recorded at the open), putting the tray in (AL11, AL12's found once put in, AL17), the emoji name registry (AL25), the `album` flag (AL39), the album's history and its pair and week tallies (AL13–AL16, AL46, AL48), the thoughtful boards (AL49), and the album's hoggie read (AL73). It owns the thoughtful packs `{ workspace, giver, recipient, week, message, holdsSticker, at, openedAt? }`, written once at their grant and marked opened once, unique per giver, recipient and the giver's Monday-start week as `givingWeekOf` reckons it from the kudos day; the kudosGiven subscriber `grantThoughtfulPacks`, which the bus schedules only for a message whose gate decision says thoughtful and which grants at most 50 of the message's recipients per mutation, each mutation scheduling the next, never in the giving mutation; one insert-only grant record per message and recipient `{ giver, message, recipient, outcome }`, whose outcome is `new` when the pack holds the recipient's sticker, `held` when the giver's album already holds them, `waiting` when an earlier pack of the giver holds it and `nextWeek` when the giver already earned that recipient's pack this week, so no pack is made; the told marker, one per message, written by the first page that finds a grant record for every recipient, which publishes `thoughtfulPacksGranted` `{ giver, message, outcomes }` once, which Analytics projects as `packs earned` with `packs_earned`, the outcomes other than `nextWeek`, and `recipients`; one thoughtful award tally per giver and Monday-start week, to which each page of the grant adds the grant records it writes, so a retried or redelivered page adds nothing; the **album stickers** `{ member, person, from, pack?, at }`, unique per member and person and never removed, which a pack's grant reads to decide its sticker and which `startAlbumHistory` records `from: "history"` once per workspace for every giver and the people they gave a kudos award to before its start instant, the history record's own creation, stored once, so being thanked never adds anyone and a member's own face needs no record; the history pages the workspace's awards 50 a mutation and then its hugs one a mutation with at most 50 of each hug's people, a hug with more carried to the next mutation, each page scheduling the next, and it is done only once every hug's people are tallied; a second start changes nothing; the pair tallies, one per member and person, `{ firstThankedAt, kudosGiven, kudosReceived, hugsGiven, hugsReceived, best }`, where the kudos are summed both ways, the hugs counted per person a hug reaches, and `best` is the thoughtful kudos the member gave the person with the most words, ties to the newest, kept as the message it names; the week tallies, one per member and Monday-start week of the award's or the hug's day, with the kudos given and received, the thoughtful awards given and received, the hugs given and received, the people thanked, thanking, hugged and hugging, each at most 12 with the rest counted, the kudos of the week among the kudos the member gave or received that week, most hugs, then most words, then the newest, and the awards and hugs of each day; both kept by the kudosGiven subscriber `tallyKudosGiven`, 50 awards a mutation, and the hugGiven subscriber `tallyHugGiven`, 50 people a mutation, filled for the past by the history's pages, and marked per award and per hug and person so each is counted once whoever reaches it first; and the emoji name registry `{ workspace, emoji, person?, name }`, unique per workspace on the emoji and on the name and never rewritten, whose shared names, the level emojis 5, 10, 15, 20 and 25, the five milestones' gold names and every company team's name, are reserved the first time a workspace's registry is used and, for a company team the copy holds later, by `reserveTeamNamesAfterTheSync` after each company sync in every workspace whose registry is in use, and whose hype names are allocated at a person's first thoughtful pack from the slug of their full name as the directory holds it, else their display name, after reserving any company team whose name's slug ends in `-hype`, so a sync whose reservation hasn't run yet can't lose a team's name to a hype name; each name is the slug of its source with the first free number where it is taken or a standard emoji name, the numbering kept per stem so an allocation costs a few index ranges. The website's `openThoughtfulPack` opens one of the member's own unopened packs while `album` and `collection` are on for them, both read without an exposure, and no workspace launch waits, in one mutation: it draws the pack's hoggie through `dropFor` with the crate key `thoughtful:{pack ID}` (AL7) and surface `website`, which pays band version 2's 15 XP and no coins while `packs` is on (AL9), marks the pack opened, and stores the pack's sticker, only where its grant decided it holds one, and then its hoggie, with the drop's rarity and whether it was the member's first of that hoggie, as the member's tray items `{ member, pack, at, putInAt? }`, answering the items and the bonus; a second open, another member's pack, and an open while `album` or `collection` is off answer refused and write nothing, so the pack keeps waiting. Packs, grant records, the told marker, the thoughtful award tallies, names, history stickers and the pair and week tallies are recorded whatever `album` says (AL3, AL14). The `album` flag is a member decision off by default, read without exposure and without writing by `albumShownTo`, so a mutation decides it as a query does; the website's `getAlbum` answers `{ kind: "off" }` while it is off for the signed-in member and, while it is on, the member's name, person and face, read from the stored company face or the sign-in face and never from the whole directory, their level, the people their album holds with each one's name and stored face, at most 500 with `morePeople`, their waiting packs, the unopened ones oldest first, at most 50 with `morePacks`, or none while a workspace launch waits, when every open refuses, and their tray, the items not yet put in, in opening order, at most 100 with `moreTray` (AL10). The website's `putIn` puts one item of the member's own tray in and `putAllIn` at most 25 of them in the put-in order, each while `album` is on for them, read without an exposure, answering the items placed, and `putAllIn` whether more wait: a hoggie is marked put in and `Hoggies` counts it found from then (AL12); a sticker is marked put in and records the album sticker `{ member, person, from: "pack", pack, at }`, unless the album already holds the person, and publishes `stickerPutIn` `{ member, person }` for each sticker it records, which Analytics projects as `album sticker collected` with no property (AL17); an item put in already, or of another member, places nothing. The put-in order is every hoggie the tray holds in opening order, read 26 at most, then, once at most 25 of them wait, every waiting sticker grouped by the first of the member's team pages in sidebar order whose people hold the sticker's person, in opening order within a page, the people on no page last; the team pages are the company teams, each with its people matched through the stored company matches other than the member, how many the album holds and how many there are, in sidebar order: the member's own teams first, then complete pages, then the most held, then by name, each call recomputing the order over the tray and the album as they are then (AL11, AL42). The website's `getAlbumHoggies` (AL73) answers `{ kind: "off" }` the same way and, while `album` is on, the signed-in member's hoggie collection read from `Hoggies`' collection whatever the Book, `achievements` or `unlocks` say: every hoggie of the current catalogue in its order with its name, rarity and art, found or still hidden, its count, its spares (every copy after the first) and the date it was first found, the hoggies found and the catalogue's total, each rarity's chance with its hoggies found and in all, and the rarest finds, at most five found hoggies of the highest rarity first and the newest found first within a rarity; a hoggie from a thoughtful pack counts only once it is put in, as `Hoggies` counts it (AL12). The website's `getThoughtfulBoards` (AL49) answers `{ kind: "off" }` the same way and, while `album` is on, the boards of the signed-in member's week as the leaderboard reckons it from their time zone: the people board, every current member on the boards ranked by the thoughtful kudos awards they gave that week, each with their name, their stored company face else their Slack picture, and the company teams of their stored match, and the team board, every company team ranked by the awards its matched members on the boards gave, ties sharing a rank as on the leaderboard and nobody at nothing ranked; the member's own row and every team of theirs are pinned, unranked while they are at nothing, and their own row is left out while their `Show me on the leaderboards` is off; a `team` argument narrows the people board to that team's givers, each keeping their rank. It reads the week's tallies in one index range and three reads per giver, never the whole directory, so a week of 1,000 givers stays within Convex's limits and about 1,300 givers in one workspace's week is its ceiling. Only this module writes packs, grant records, told markers, thoughtful award tallies, album stickers, the history, the pair and week tallies, tray items and emoji names.
