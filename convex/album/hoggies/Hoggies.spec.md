# Hoggies

Hoggies: the hoggies and team crests members collect from the crates they open (CL1–CL26, decision 25), knowing nothing about why a crate was opened. It owns the versioned hoggie catalogue, version 1 being every hoggie in the pinned `@posthog/brand` 0.13.0 with one rarity each, common, uncommon, rare, epic or legendary, weighted 50, 28, 14, 6 and 2, and version 2 adding the package's 54 team crests, each with a frozen roster of two to four version 1 hoggies, every hoggie on exactly one; the draw, a pure function of the deployment's `HOGGIE_DRAW_SECRET` (the empty string where it is unset), the member, the crate key and the catalogue version, through SHA-256, whose first 4 bytes pick the rarity by cumulative weight, among only the rarities at or above the floor and at or below the cap its caller passes where it passes them, by the weights its caller names (`base`, today's, or `kudos`, a kudos pack's under `rarity-caps`: 50, 28, 18, 3.5 and 0.5), and whose next 4 pick the hoggie in catalogue order; a draw that names its weights never drops a rarity because the member owns all of it, and within the drawn rarity a draw handed the member's hoggies prefers one they don't own and draws any once they own them all, while a draw that names none skips every rarity whose hoggies the member owns, as before (HH39, HH40, HH42, `spec/hugs-hook.md`); the `collection` flag, a member decision off by default, read without exposure everywhere but the drop; the `crests` flag, a member decision off by default, read without exposure everywhere but an open that completes or answers a crest; the pack bands (CV3), versioned catalogue data, version 1 giving the weekly, group, Journey, feature and bought kinds 5 to 25 XP in steps of 5 and 1 to 5 coins, and version 2 adding the `thoughtful` kind of thoughtful packs, whose crate key is `thoughtful:{pack ID}`, at exactly 15 XP and no coins (AL7, AL9), while the `level` kind and the `welcome` kind of the welcome item, whose one crate key is `welcome:first`, have no band and pay nothing (LP11); the pack draw (CV4), a pure function of the same secret, the member, the crate key and the version that set the crate kind's band, so version 2 changes no earlier kind's bonus, through SHA-256 of `{secret}:{member}:{crate}:pack:{version}`, whose first 4 bytes pick the XP step and next 4 the coins, each uniformly within the band and independently of the hoggie; the `packs` flag, a member decision off by default, read without exposure everywhere but the drop; and each member's insert-only drop records, unique per member and crate key, with the hoggie, its rarity at the draw, the member's drop sequence `n`, the hoggie's `copy`, the catalogue version, the open's time, from version 2 the crest the drop completed or null, and the pack bonus its open paid with the member's standing with coins before and after it, beside a tally per member and hoggie and one per member, written in the drop's mutation so no read counts the history; a drop whose caller passes `waits`, which only the album's old open of a thoughtful pack does (LP11), waits in the member's tray instead (AL12): it stores a tray drop `{ member, crate, putInAt?, crest? }` beside its drop record, leaves the hoggie's tally alone and decides no crest, its `copy` counting the copies found by then, until `putDropIn`, which Album calls in the mutation that puts the hoggie in, marks the tray drop put in once and counts the hoggie found from that instant under the next drop sequence number, so a find's sequence follows the order hoggies are found, and decides there the crest the find completes, kept on the tray drop since a drop record never changes, so every count read, the crests and their dates, the avatar's holding and `dropsByDecision` find it only once it is put in; `dropsByDecision` reads the waiting crates once and leaves them out, answers a put-in drop with the crest its put-in decided and at its open's place and time, and `hoggiesOf`'s newest find is the newest found drop or the hoggie put in last, whichever came later; a thoughtful drop stored before tray drops existed, with none, was counted at its open and stays found. The turn of a thoughtful pack's item passes no `waits`, so its drop is found at once, counted and its crest decided at the turn like any other open's (LP11). Its one write, `dropFor`, called by Quests and Unlocks in the mutation of a crate's first successful open of a weekly quest, a group quest, a Journey step or a feature (CL6), by Album in a thoughtful pack's open (AL8), and by Unlocks for each of the crates `level:{level}:1` to `level:{level}:3` in the mutation that records a level up, the third with a rare floor, after the open's own writes and before the open's event, reads `collection` with exposure, draws, then reads `packs` with exposure and, while it is on, pays the crate's pack bonus, except for a level up's crates, which pay none and read nothing of `packs`, through `applyGain` (CV5), stores the drop and its tallies with the crest it completed whatever `crests` says and the bonus it paid, and publishes hoggieDropped with the bonus's XP and coins and the surface its caller passed, `slack` or `website`, never inferred from the crate (MS12), or does nothing where the crate already dropped or `collection` is off; `packOf` answers the bonus a crate's drop paid, with its standings, read from the drop record; `waitingPacksOf` answers the member's waiting packs (CV7): Quests' pending crates through one index range of at most 51 rows, oldest first while the group quest applies to the member and only the weekly ones in week order otherwise, and its finished and unopened Journey steps, while Quests applies to the member and each step's own predicate holds; Unlocks' ready features while the unlock path shows to the member and the feature ships to them; and the member's bought crates not opened yet, through one index range of at most 51 rows; ordered by when each became waiting (the pending crate's creation, the Journey reward's time, the feature's `readyAt`, the bought crate's `boughtAt`), then by crate key with its numbers in numeric order, the first 50 numbered `n` from 1, with `morePacks` true where more wait, and no amount, read through each module's own reads and never their tables; `dropOf` answers a crate's stored drop and `dropsByDecision` a member's drops newest written first, both read from the drop records. `collectionOf` answers every catalogue hoggie with the member's count and first find, each rarity's chance, held and catalogue counts and, while `crests` is shown, every crest with its roster, the roster hoggies held and when it was collected, at the first find of the roster hoggie with the highest drop sequence, never stored as its own record, read from the member's tallies through one index range of at most one row per catalogue hoggie, never the drop records; `hoggiesOf` answers App Home how many distinct catalogue entries the member holds, the catalogue's size, their newest drop and, while `crests` is shown, how many crests they collected, read from those tallies and the one newest drop record. It also sells crates for coins (CL27–CL31): `buyCrate`, reached through Commands, answers null and writes nothing while `collection` or the `crate-store` flag (a member decision off by default, read with exposure only there) is off for the member; it answers a crate number the member already bought as bought again and writes nothing, so a double click or a retried request buys once, refuses any other number but the member's bought crates so far plus one as stale, then refuses while coins aren't visible to the member, then spends the catalogue's price of 50 coins through the wallet's spend step for `{ crate }` (refusing no-coins, writing nothing), and stores the bought crate `bought:{n}` with its number and time and publishes crateBought, all in one mutation; `openCrate` opens only a bought crate of the member's that isn't opened yet, only while `collection` is on, drawing its drop as `dropFor` does and marking the crate opened, so a crate opened while `collection` is off keeps waiting, while turning `crate-store` off stops only buying. `waitingCratesOf` answers the member's unopened bought crates and `crateStoreOf` the price and the next number while coins are visible. It also keeps each member's avatar (#537): `setAvatar`, reached through Commands, does nothing while `collection` or the `hoggie-avatar` flag (a member decision off by default, read with exposure only there) is off for the member, and otherwise stores the hoggie as the member's one avatar, or removes it for none, only where the member holds a tally of that hoggie, refusing any other and changing nothing, so a repeat leaves the same state; `avatarShownOn` answers a viewer a member's avatar, the hoggie with its catalogue name, rarity and art, or null, only while both flags are on for the viewer and for the member, read without an exposure, and nothing otherwise, and `avatarChoiceOf` answers App Home the member's avatar and every hoggie they hold, in catalogue order. It also keeps each member's pet hoggie (core-loop LP57–LP60): `makePetOf`, reached from the website's makePet, stores the hoggie as the member's one pet, one row per member keyed by its workspace and member, only where the member holds a tally of it, refusing any other and changing nothing, so a repeat leaves the same state and a later pick replaces it, with no default pet; `petOf` answers the member's pet and `petsIn` every pet in a workspace, each with its catalogue name and rarity, through one index range; the `pet-hoggie` flag, a member decision off by default, is read without an exposure everywhere, for the viewer for a pet's display and for the owner for a pick. Only this module writes drop records, tray drops, tallies, bought crates, avatars and pets.
