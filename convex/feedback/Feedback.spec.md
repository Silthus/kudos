the one public reply per kudos message as desired state, whose sections modules set and whose routing (the announcement channel and muted notices) feedback decides, showing a level section only while `xp-levels` is on in the workspace decision, even for a member who previews it; one public notice per member and level for a level a reward record reaches, which has no kudos message and so goes only to the announcement channel, and nowhere while none is picked or while `quests`, the feature that pays every reward, doesn't apply in the workspace decision, even for a member who previews it, checked again when Slack takes the notice (F18); App Home as desired state, published again for a member after each xpRecorded about them while `xp-levels` is on in their member decision, so it never shows XP from before the record, after Unlocks makes a feature ready for them, for every admin after the announcement channel changes, after a weekly quest's progress changes what it shows, and after a role change or a click, and again when what it shows changed while it was sent: one homeSelections row per member holds the tab and leaderboard period they last picked, and its tab bar shows each tab only while its own flag is on in the member's decision, unless named otherwise, Leaderboard while `everyone-board` is on and, while `xp-levels` and `unlocks` are on, only once the member has opened the Leaderboard, Admin with the announcement channel picker for workspace admins and owners while `xp-levels` is on in the workspace decision, since the channel is shared, Settings while either is on, with the leaderboard switch only while `everyone-board` is on and muting notices and DMs only while `xp-levels` is on, and Profile beside any of them, with the member's allowance and kudos given and received and, while `xp-levels` is on, their level, XP to the next level and skill points saved from level 5, and while `unlocks` is on too, the skill points only once the skill tree is open, each ready feature with what it opens and an Open button, and the next shipped feature still locked, and where Quests applies the current week's quests once Quests is opened, each with its goal, its progress toward its target and its reward, or done with an Open button until its crate is opened, and the Journey's activated steps, each active with its goal, its reward and, for a step that has one, the condition in words, or done with an Open button until its crate is opened; and the copy every reply is rendered from: the private replies, the welcome message, the level-up DM (which names saved skill points, for the skill tree, only from level 5, and while `unlocks` is on only once the member has opened the skill tree, with each feature the level up made ready and what it opens, as App Home does), the public level notice, App Home, the `/hedgehug` replies, and the rows of a board with its improvement line, all naming the product HedgeHug.
