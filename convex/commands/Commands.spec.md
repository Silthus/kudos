# Commands

Commands: the one platform-neutral registry every App Home click is routed through (D17). A command shows a Home tab (showHomeTab) or a leaderboard period (showLeaderboard), turns `Show me on the leaderboards` on or off (setLeaderboardVisibility), mutes notices or turns DMs off or on (setProgressSettings), or sets or clears the announcement channel (setAnnouncementChannel, for workspace admins and owners only, refused for anyone else); it acts on the clicking member only, is applied by the module that owns its state, and sets that state to the value it carries, so a second click on the same button changes nothing, and the adapter runs each click once, keyed by the payload's idempotency key. Each command runs only while its own flag is on: showLeaderboard and setLeaderboardVisibility behind `everyone-board`, setProgressSettings and setAnnouncementChannel behind `xp-levels`, and showHomeTab behind the flag of the tab it shows; after the announcement channel changes, every admin's App Home is published again; a command whose flag is off changes nothing, and a candidate the registry does not know, such as a removed switch, is no command at all.
