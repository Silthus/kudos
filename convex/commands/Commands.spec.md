# Commands

Commands: the one platform-neutral registry every App Home click and every Open in a DM is routed through (D17). A command shows a Home tab (showHomeTab) or a leaderboard period (showLeaderboard), turns `Show me on the leaderboards` on or off (setLeaderboardVisibility), mutes notices or turns DMs off or on (setProgressSettings), sets or clears the announcement channel (setAnnouncementChannel, for workspace admins and owners only, refused for anyone else), opens a ready, shipped feature for good (openUnlock, applied by Unlocks, which refuses anything else), or opens a finished quest's crate (openQuestCrate, applied by Quests, naming the quest, a Journey step or a weekly quest by its week and slot, and never a member, and refused unless the clicking member's reward record for it is stored), or swaps one unfinished weekly quest of the clicking member's current week (rerollQuest, applied by Quests, naming the weekly quest by its week and slot, and refused after the week's one swap), or buys a perk of the skill tree (buyPerk, applied by Progression with the click's trigger as its idempotency key, naming the perk by its ID); it acts on the clicking member only, is applied by the module that owns its state, and sets that state to the value it carries, so a second click on the same button changes nothing, and the adapter runs each click once, keyed by the payload's idempotency key. Each command runs only while its own flag is on: showLeaderboard behind `everyone-board` and, while `xp-levels` and `unlocks` are on, the member having opened the Leaderboard, setLeaderboardVisibility behind `everyone-board`, setProgressSettings and setAnnouncementChannel behind `xp-levels`, openUnlock behind `xp-levels` and `unlocks`, openQuestCrate and rerollQuest where Quests applies, buyPerk where the skill tree applies, and showHomeTab behind the flag of the tab it shows; after the announcement channel changes, every admin's App Home is published again; a command whose flag is off changes nothing, and a candidate the registry does not know, such as a removed switch, is no command at all.
