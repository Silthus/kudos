# Commands

Commands: the one platform-neutral registry every App Home click, every Open or `Buy` in a DM and every website command is routed through (D17). A command shows a Home tab (showHomeTab) or a leaderboard period (showLeaderboard), turns `Show me on the leaderboards` on or off (setLeaderboardVisibility), mutes notices or turns DMs off or on (setProgressSettings), sets or clears the announcement channel (setAnnouncementChannel, for workspace admins and owners only, refused for anyone else), opens a ready, shipped feature for good (openUnlock, applied by Unlocks, which refuses anything else), or opens a finished quest's crate (openQuestCrate, applied by Quests, naming the quest, a Journey step or a weekly quest by its week and slot, and never a member, and refused unless the clicking member's reward record for it is stored), or swaps one unfinished weekly quest of the clicking member's current week (rerollQuest, applied by Quests, naming the weekly quest by its week and slot, and refused after the week's one swap), or buys a perk of the skill tree (buyPerk, applied by the skill tree's owner with the idempotency key its entrance builds, `slack:` and the click's trigger from Slack, naming the perk by its ID, and showing the `Skill tree` tab whenever it answers an outcome, so a refused purchase's reason shows on its perk whichever tab was open, while a `Buy` it ignores, for a coin perk where coins aren't shown, selects no tab (CO14), and answering with its outcome, which the adapter tells as feedback's line only for a `Buy` clicked in a DM), or buys a kudos emoji of the store (buyItem, applied by the store with the idempotency key its entrance builds, `slack:` and the click's trigger from Slack, naming the item by its ID, and showing the `Inventory` tab whenever it answers an outcome, a refusal's line shown under the item in the App Home published after that click only), or puts an owned kudos emoji in a slot (equip, from a `Put in slot` menu, naming the item and the slot) or empties a slot (takeOff, from `Take off`), both applied by the store, storing no outcome and answering it, and selecting no tab; it acts on the clicking member only, is applied by the module that owns its state, and sets that state to the value it carries, so a second click on the same button changes nothing, and the adapter runs each click once, keyed by the payload's idempotency key. Each command runs only while its own flag is on in the clicking member's decision, unless named otherwise: showLeaderboard behind `everyone-board` and, while `xp-levels` and `unlocks` are on, the member having opened the Leaderboard, setLeaderboardVisibility behind `everyone-board`, setProgressSettings behind `xp-levels`, setAnnouncementChannel behind `xp-levels` in the workspace decision, so a preview can't change the shared channel, openUnlock behind `xp-levels` and `unlocks`, openQuestCrate and rerollQuest where Quests applies, buyPerk where the skill tree applies, buyItem, equip and takeOff where the store is shown, and showHomeTab behind the flag of the tab it shows; after the announcement channel changes, every admin's App Home is published again; a command whose flag is off changes nothing, and a candidate the registry does not know, such as a removed switch, is no command at all.
