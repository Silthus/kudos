# Commands

Commands: the one platform-neutral registry every App Home click, every Open or `Buy` in a DM and every website command is routed through (D17). A command shows a Home tab (showHomeTab) or a leaderboard period (showLeaderboard), turns `Show me on the leaderboards` on or off (setLeaderboardVisibility), mutes notices or turns DMs off or on (setProgressSettings), turns one kind of message on or off while `message-settings` shows it (setMessageSetting), sets or clears the announcement channel (setAnnouncementChannel, for workspace admins and owners only, refused for anyone else), sets the group mode with its allowlist (setGroupMode, applied by Groups, for workspace admins and owners where `groups` applies, refused for anyone else), switches the Monday post on or off or picks its time zone (setMondayPost, applied by Leaderboard, for workspace admins and owners where `monday-post` applies, refused for anyone else, switching on only while groups are available and an announcement channel is set), opens a ready, shipped feature for good (openUnlock, applied by Unlocks, which refuses anything else), or opens a finished quest's crate (openQuestCrate, applied by Quests, naming the quest, a Journey step or a weekly quest by its week and slot, and never a member, and refused unless the clicking member's reward record for it is stored), or swaps one unfinished weekly quest of the clicking member's current week (rerollQuest, applied by Quests, naming the weekly quest by its week and slot, and refused after the week's one swap), or buys a perk of the skill tree (buyPerk, applied by the skill tree's owner with the idempotency key its entrance builds, `slack:` and the click's trigger from Slack, naming the perk by its ID, and showing the `Skill tree` tab whenever it answers an outcome, so a refused purchase's reason shows on its perk whichever tab was open, while a `Buy` it ignores, for a coin perk where coins aren't shown, selects no tab (CO14), and answering with its outcome, which the adapter tells as feedback's line only for a `Buy` clicked in a DM), or buys a kudos emoji of the store (buyItem, applied by the store with the idempotency key its entrance builds, `slack:` and the click's trigger from Slack, naming the item by its ID, and showing the `Inventory` tab whenever it answers an outcome, a refusal's line shown under the item in the App Home published after that click only), or buys the member's next crate for coins (buyCrate, applied by the module that keeps the drop records, naming the crate's number, safe to repeat by that number rather than by a key, so a second click with a new trigger buys nothing more, storing no outcome and answering it, a refusal's line shown in the App Home published after that click only), or opens a crate the member bought (openCrate, applied by that module too, naming the crate's key, answering none), or makes a hoggie the member holds their avatar or goes back to none (setAvatar, applied by that module too, naming the hoggie or null, from App Home's `Use as avatar` menu or the website, storing no outcome and answering none, and dropped from a Slack click before its receipt while the avatar isn't shown to the member, so such a click, like a command main doesn't know, stores no receipt and republishes nothing), or sets which of the workspace's custom emoji the store sells (setCustomKudosEmoji, applied by the store, naming every picked emoji, for workspace admins and owners where `custom-kudos-emoji` is on in their own member decision and the store is shown to them, refused for anyone else, storing no outcome and answering none), or sets or clears the member's own link to a posthog.com profile (linkMyCompanyProfile, applied by Company profiles, naming the profile's posthog.com ID or null, from the website only, so a Slack click naming it is no command at all, deciding `team-pages` with exposure and refusing, writing nothing, a member already matched by name or by an admin, a profile another member holds and a profile not in the current copy, and answering its outcome), or sets, sets to none or clears a member's admin link to a posthog.com profile (linkCompanyProfile, applied by Company profiles, naming the member, whom the website resolves from the opaque key its Settings read gave the admin, and the profile's posthog.com ID, `none` or null, from the website only, so a Slack click naming it is no command at all, for workspace admins and owners only, deciding `team-pages` with exposure, refusing, writing nothing, anyone else, a member who isn't a current member of the clicking admin's workspace and a profile not in the current copy, deleting every other member's admin link to that profile in the same mutation, and answering its outcome), or saves the workspace's daily allowance, kudos emoji and hug budget together (setWorkspaceSettings, applied by Workspace, from the website only, so a Slack click naming it is no command at all, for the maintainer's own Slack account only, the member KUDOS_MAINTAINER names, refusing anyone else before deciding `workspace-settings`, which it then decides with exposure, refusing a value out of bounds with the setting it names, writing nothing, and answering its outcome), or picks the clicking member's primary group (pickPrimaryGroup, applied by Groups, naming the group and the surface it was picked on, `welcome` or `settings`, ignoring its idempotency key since a repeat leaves the same state, and recording primary group picked only when the pick is stored); it acts on the clicking member only, is applied by the module that owns its state, and sets that state to the value it carries, so a second click on the same button changes nothing, and the adapter runs each click once, keyed by the payload's idempotency key. Every command runs with the surface it came from beside its idempotency key, `slack` from the Slack adapter and `website` from a website command, and openUnlock, openQuestCrate and openCrate pass it on to the hoggie their open drops (MS12). Each command runs only while its own flag is on in the clicking member's decision, unless named otherwise: showLeaderboard behind `everyone-board` and, while `xp-levels` and `unlocks` are on, the member having opened the Leaderboard, setLeaderboardVisibility behind `everyone-board`, setProgressSettings behind `xp-levels`, setAnnouncementChannel behind `xp-levels` in the workspace decision, so a preview can't change the shared channel, openUnlock behind `xp-levels` and `unlocks`, openQuestCrate and rerollQuest where Quests applies, buyPerk where the skill tree applies, buyItem where the store is shown, pickPrimaryGroup where groups are shown to the member, and showHomeTab behind the flag of the tab it shows; after the announcement channel, the group mode, the Monday post or the custom kudos emoji change, every admin's App Home is published again; a command whose flag is off changes nothing, and a candidate the registry does not know, such as a removed switch, is no command at all.
