# Hugs

Hugs: a light thank-you by reaction (SR1–SR10, SR12–SR16, SR20, SR21, SR24), never an award. The Slack adapter hands it a normalized reaction, the workspace, the giver and the message's author as chat user IDs, the conversation, the message's ts, the emoji reacted and the reaction's time, and nothing else of the event; decideReaction decides it in the mutation that records the event's receipt, after the adapter's mayReactWith query let it through: a reaction 24 hours or more after the message, or before it, does nothing; a giver or author the directory doesn't know answers unknown people for the adapter to look up, and the reaction is decided again as at its time once they are known; a giver or author who isn't a member does nothing; a reaction whose emoji is the workspace's kudos emoji or an earned emoji the giver holds, read through the album's earned emoji check by the emoji's name, is a hug; one whose emoji the album's hypeEmojiFor resolves through the emoji registry to a member's hype emoji that the giver neither owns nor holds is a hype reaction, which writes nothing in Hugs and, while the giver's `hugs` decision is on, publishes hypeReacted with the giver, the emoji's owner, the message's author, the emoji and the message (HH19, HH20); any other reaction does nothing, and mayReactWith answers so before any mutation; on a Given kudos message, read through the engine's exported reads (its author and the giver's own award on it, unique lookups), the hug is for its recipients, the author hugging it does nothing, and a recipient hugging it hugs it back, for its author alone as a hug on the author's own message is (HH19); on any other message it is for the author, never the giver. A giver hugs a message once, and gives at most their hug budget of hugs on their day (R12), 10 unless the workspace row sets hugBudget, which a maintainer sets with an internal mutation; the once-check, the budget count and the insert-only hug record are read and written in that one mutation, so a hug past the budget writes nothing and the same member can hug that message on a later day within its 24 hours. Only a reaction that passes every other check reads the giver's `hugs` member decision, with exposure, and does nothing while it is off. A recorded hug publishes hugGiven, which names the author when the hug is for one person and the emoji reacted without its skin tone and carries `rewards`, the giver's `hug-rewards` member decision read with exposure in the hug's mutation (HH34), and progression, following it, pays the giver 1 XP keyed `hug-given:{conversation}:{ts}` a moment after the hug (HH23); payHugPeople, its subscriber, then pays each of its people, 100 recipients a mutation from the engine's paged read of the message's awards and continuing from its cursor, skipping a recipient who is no longer a member, and writes one row per hug and person, so a rerun pays nobody twice; after writing a person's row it publishes hugReceived with that person's own week and `rewards`, that person's `hug-rewards` member decision read with exposure in that page (HH34), so each person is published once for a hug, and progression, following it, pays that person 1 XP keyed `hug:{giver}:{conversation}:{ts}`. Hugs writes no reward record. The hug's reward records carry no cause, no coins and no item, at catalogue version `hugs-1`; the people are paid whatever their own flag says. Hugs sends no message, ephemeral, DM or reaction for a hug and never calls Slack: hugGiven says the hug is the giver's first when the hugs' giver index held no earlier hug of theirs, the hug's own record being the once-marker, and the welcome module answers that first hug (core-loop LP73, MQ14, MQ15); and nothing that reads awards sees one, apart from the album's tallies, which count hugs beside kudos and never as kudos counts. For the activity (SR11) it exports hugsGivenByDecision, a giver's hugs newest recorded first with the people each is for, the author or the Given kudos message's recipients through the engine's read of the message's awards, and hugsReceivedByDecision, one row per message whose hugs paid the member, which payHugPeople writes as a new version each time it pays them, the one before marked superseded, with its hugs, its newest five paid givers, the emoji of its newest and its newest hug's instant, read newest written first from a position down on one index, the versions current at a snapshot's creation time or, without one, the current versions, and hugsShownTo, the giver's `hugs` decision read without an exposure. The hug's writes keep its counts (SR14): the hug's mutation adds it, with its XP, to the giver's row for their week (Monday to Sunday of their day) and to the message's all-time hug count; payHugPeople adds it, with its XP, to each person's row for their own week at the hug's time, and to that person's count for the message that week with the time of its last hug, from which the row keeps the week's most-hugged message, the latest last hug winning a tie; the row's hug counts and XP are still written, so a rollback finds them, but nothing reads them: the recap, the Monday DM's hugs section and the hugs recap sent report read the week's hugs given and received, and their XP, from `hugXpOf` for the row's week, which reads them from Leaderboard's `totalsOf` (HH30). A week's row starts waiting for its recap (SR12), and the Monday DM delivers it: its hourly sweep pages through the rows of this week and last that wait for it, 100 a mutation, and ensures the member's Monday DM for the Monday after the row's week, so every member with a week of hugs has one, at 09:00 in their time zone. At that DM's start, hugsSectionFor reads the member's week before that Monday and marks the row the Monday DM's while it is waiting or scheduled with no try begun, so a week the standalone recap began before the Monday DM took over is never sent again; a recap job queued before then sends nothing. The section is left out for someone no longer a member, for whom `xp-levels` is off, whose `hugs-recap` place isn't `dm`, or, read last with exposure, for whom `hugs` is off. Otherwise it holds SR12's copy as call 10 words it, each line naming the hugs with the workspace's kudos emoji and saying reacted, a zero count left out, `1 hug` singular and the most-hugged line only when they received hugs and the workspace URL is known, linked by its permalink with the thread root of a kudos posted as a thread reply, the closing line `Hugs are a quick thanks. A kudos, a message that mentions a teammate with :<kudos emoji>:, earns much more XP.` with the workspace's kudos emoji (HH14), which the Monday DM's hugs section closes on too when it carries no quests, and which gives way to the quests section beside them, as the Monday quests spec's DM with hugs says, and the message settings footer while `message-settings` is on. A Monday DM Slack took reports it as hugs recap sent with only the hugs given and received, their XP and via `monday` (SR16, MQ27); it goes out at most once, as the Monday DM does.

## invariants
- a member hugs a message at most once: A giver's reactions on one message make one hug, which pays its author or recipients and the giver 1 XP once in all and reaches PostHog as one hug given, whether the event is redelivered, the reaction is removed and added again or given again with another skin tone.
  over: a redelivered event, a removed and re-added reaction and a second skin tone on an ordinary message, and a redelivered hug on a Given kudos message with two recipients
  via: SR5 SR23: a member hugs a message at most once
  refuted: giveHug no longer looked for the giver's hug on the message -> a re-added reaction and a second skin tone each made a second hug and a second hug given (2026-10-16)
  because: a hug is a one-click thank-you worth 1 XP each way; paying it again for every redelivery, re-add or skin tone would let a member farm XP for themselves and a teammate from one message (SR5)
  kinds: state
  checklist: revalidated-permission dismissed: no permission is involved; the hug's mutation reads the giver's membership and the message's awards when it decides
  checklist: separation-of-duties dismissed: no approval policy applies to a hug
  checklist: legal-state-succession declared as a member hugs a message at most once
  checklist: supersession-safety dismissed: a hug record is insert-only, unique per message and giver, and a later reaction finds it in its own transaction
  checklist: worker-fencing dismissed: no worker owns a hug; the decision is one mutation and the people's pages are keyed per hug and person
  checklist: commit-ordered-effects dismissed: a hug sends nothing to Slack; its hugGiven commits with the record, and its XP follows hugGiven under the hug's key
  checklist: resumption-coverage dismissed: payHugPeople continues from the recipients' cursor, and a page rerun skips each person whose row exists and each reward whose key is recorded
- a hug never passes the hug budget: A giver's hugs on their day never pay past their hug budget, 10 unless the workspace sets another, even when two reactions arrive at once for the last hug left.
  over: eleven hugs on one day, the twelfth the next day, a workspace budget of 2, and two reactions at once after nine hugs
  via: SR6 SR23: a hug never passes the hug budget
  refuted: giveHug refused a hug only when the hugs left went below 0 instead of at 0 -> 3 of 3 tests failed: the giver got 11 XP from 11 hugs, both concurrent reactions paid past the 10th, and a budget of 2 paid 3 (2026-10-16)
  because: the budget keeps hugs light (SR6); a check and a write in separate transactions would let two reactions at once both take the tenth hug
  kinds: state
  checklist: revalidated-permission dismissed: no permission is involved; the budget is counted in the mutation that records the hug
  checklist: separation-of-duties dismissed: no approval policy applies to a hug
  checklist: legal-state-succession declared as a hug never passes the hug budget
  checklist: supersession-safety dismissed: Convex serializes two mutations that read the same giver's hugs that day, so the second sees the first's record or retries
  checklist: worker-fencing dismissed: no worker holds the budget; each reaction's mutation counts and records on its own
  checklist: commit-ordered-effects dismissed: a hug past the budget writes and sends nothing; a hug within it commits its record and hugGiven together, and its XP follows hugGiven
  checklist: resumption-coverage dismissed: no batch counts the budget; a looked-up hug is decided again in one mutation as at its time
- a hug is never an award: A hug changes no award, board, kudos total, allowance, coin, quest, Journey step, sticker or gate decision; it writes only its own records, reward records and XP, its flags' exposures, Leaderboard's hugs given and hugs received beside the kudos it ranks (HH26), and in the album's pair and week tallies the hug counts, the hugged lists, the day counts and the kudos-of-the-week ranking hugs decide, never their kudos counts.
  over: three hugs, on a Given kudos message and on ordinary messages, by members who each gave and got kudos, with every feature on, compared table by table with the state before them
  via: SR2 SR23: a hug is never an award
  refuted: recordHug paid the giver's hug reward 1 coin instead of none -> three givers' wallets each read 1 coin above the state before the hugs (2026-10-16)
  because: hugs sit below kudos on purpose (SR2): a hug that reached awards would count on boards and kudos totals, spend or refill no allowance yet earn coins, finish quests and Journey steps, or reach the meaningful-thank-you classifier
  kinds: state
  checklist: revalidated-permission dismissed: no permission is involved; a hug reads awards only to find its people
  checklist: separation-of-duties dismissed: no approval policy applies to a hug
  checklist: legal-state-succession declared as a hug is never an award
  checklist: supersession-safety dismissed: a hug writes no award-side row, so no award-side state can be displaced by it
  checklist: worker-fencing dismissed: no worker owns a hug's writes
  checklist: commit-ordered-effects dismissed: hugGiven reaches payHugPeople, analytics and the album's tallyHugGiven, which reads a kudos message's awards only to find its people and its week, and counts the hug, never an award
  checklist: resumption-coverage dismissed: payHugPeople pays only reward records keyed per hug and person, whatever page it resumes from
