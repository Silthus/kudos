# Quests

Quests: owns each member's quests on the Monday path (spec/monday-quests.md, ADR-0004 as amended, ADR-0011, ADR-0013): three onboarding quests, then three drawn quests a week. The unlock path's Journey, weekly quests, swaps, quest crates, held rewards and group quest are gone; the rows they stored stay in their tables unread, and the Journey's step IDs live on as the onboarding quests' reward keys. A member is on the Monday path while `monday-quest` and xp-levels are on in their member decision; `startQuests` writes a member's start once, and Quests' own kudosGiven subscriber calls it, after reading `monday-quest` with an exposure, for a giver with no start, whatever the kudos says, before counting that kudos, while the hourly sweep starts the quests, after reading `monday-quest` with an exposure, of a member who has a `welcomes` row and no start. Off the Monday path Quests records nothing for the member: no start, draw, counted award or finish, and its kudosGiven subscriber moves nothing. The three onboarding quests (`first-thanks`, paid for the earliest kudos the member gave since their start that is still waiting for Quests (not judged, recorded within progressionWaitedFor), else for the kudos being handled, `someone-new`, counted only for a kudos in another message than the one `first-thanks` was paid for, whichever order the kudos are delivered in, each reply naming the quests whose reward its own kudos caused, the same when Quests handles that kudos again, and `two-days`, counting the days of any kudos, with no Book dependency) are active together from the start, none of them asks a kudos to say why (MQ46, MQ47), and they are finished by their rows in Quests' finish record alone, which `nextOnboardingQuestOf` reads; each pays 50 XP with no coins from catalogue version monday-1, and a step finished before keeps its record and pays nothing again. Each onboarding quest counts a kudos as of when it was given, whatever order the subscribers run in: `someone-new` a kudos to someone new in any message but the one `first-thanks` was paid for, and a leave only after `first-thanks` finished, `two-days` only the kudos given up to it. `someone-new` also finishes whenever every current member besides the member has been thanked by them, checked on each memberLeft the directory publishes, for every member of the workspace whose step is unfinished, a page of 100 members per mutation, each checked in a mutation of its own, and on each memberRejoined for the member who came back, so a small or shrinking workspace never strands it. A finish writes its finish row and publishes questFinished with its reward key, reward, catalogue version and cause in one mutation and pays nothing itself: `Progression` records the XP and `Album` earns one face-down hoggie item keyed `quest:<reward key>` with reason quest from that fact (HH23, HH24); Quests sends no DM of its own, and adds one Quest done line with its XP and an unidentified Hoggie to the giver's after-kudos reply, which waits for Quests on the Monday path. The weekly draw opens once the member has a start and all three onboarding quests are finished, at the next trigger after the last of them (the hourly sweep or a later kudos, never the kudos that finished it), and then at each week's start: the member's week starts Monday 00:00 in their time zone, or in UTC without one, and is keyed by that Monday. It draws from pool version monday-1, whose six quests count any kudos and none asks it to say why (HH13): `kudos-once` (a kudos to someone), `kudos-two-days` (kudos on 2 different days), `three-in-a-day` (3 people in one day) and three breadth quests, at 20 XP each with no coins, and offers no swap. The draw ranks the pool by a hash of member, week and quest and takes the first 3 that are feasible, checked against stored facts at the draw: people left to meet a goal (every current member besides the member for anyone, those the member never thanked, those not thanked in the 30 days before), days left in the week, counting the draw's day only while the member has kudos left that day, for people in one day a daily allowance that holds them and either that many kudos left that day or another day left in the week (HH13), and recognitions left that week, at most one per person and day within each day's kudos (P16); with fewer feasible, it draws fewer, and with none it stores nothing, so a later draw that week can still give 3. It stores the draw with its week, its time and each slot's quest, reward, catalogue version and the facts that made it feasible, publishes questsDrawn naming the member, the week, the drawn quests by their pool IDs and the draw's time in the same mutation, and republishes App Home. A week after the one the draws opened in is drawn from the facts at the week's start, stored with the week's start as its time whenever it runs, by the hourly sweep, which pages through the members, 100 per mutation, and schedules one draw for each member on the Monday path whose draws are open and who has none for their current week, plus one mutation for the next page, so no mutation schedules more than Convex allows, or by the kudosGiven subscriber for the giver, before it counts the award, so the award that drew the week counts. The same subscriber counts the drawn quests: an award counts toward a slot of the giver's Monday draw for the week of the award's day only when it was made at or after the draw's time and the slot's quest counts it, decided by the pure quest progress function from the stored gate decision read from the event (P12) and from when the giver last thanked the recipient before that award, in the order awards were recorded; each counted award is stored once per member, week, slot and award and never changed, so a redelivered kudosGiven, an edit or a retry never changes progress already counted (P84), and a week drawn on the unlock path waits as it is. A slot's progress is what its quest counts of those awards up to its target; when it reaches the target, the quest finishes the Monday path's way, with the slot's pool quest named on questFinished, and a finished slot counts nothing more. For the activity it answers the quests one kudos message finished for its giver from the Monday-path reward records with that cause, each with its goal and reward XP, and for the website's quests panel it gives the member's onboarding quests and this week's drawn quests.
