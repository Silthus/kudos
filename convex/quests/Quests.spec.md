# Quests

Quests: owns each member's quests and the Journey (P80 to P93, ADR-0004 as amended). The Journey is versioned catalogue data keyed by step ID, never by number, and holds only steps whose feature has shipped: `first-thanks`, active from the start, `first-quest` (finish a weekly quest, 20 XP), active once the member opens Quests, `someone-new` (thank someone you haven't thanked before, 30 XP), active once the member opens the Leaderboard, `two-days` (give kudos that say why on 2 different days, 30 XP), active once the member opens the Book, and `your-emoji` (give a kudos that says why with one of your kudos emoji, 40 XP and 20 coins), from catalogue version 2. `two-days` exists only where the Book applies too, by Achievements' predicate, read in every Journey path it touches: where it doesn't, its opening, the join and App Home's publish activate nothing, kudos count nothing toward it, questsOf and App Home's Journey lines leave it out, its finished DM is checked at delivery and says nothing of it, and a stale Open on its crate writes nothing (P78). `your-emoji` applies to a member only while the store is visible to them and their Journey has started: a predicate of the member, never of the workspace, read alike by its activation, its progress, App Home's and the website's Journey, its finished DM at delivery, the success reply when Slack takes it, the website's activity and its crate, so where it doesn't apply it is silent and unlisted and a reward it recorded stays recorded (ST19). Its activation is stored once, with no DM, at the moment the first of Quests' unlockOpened subscriber for Coins, App Home's publish or the kudosGiven subscriber finds it applies and unactivated, never at an older opening of Coins or an older kudos; the join activates it at the moment for a member who had opened Coins by then where it applies. The first kudosGiven whose stored gate decision says why and whose loadout emoji isn't empty finishes it, activating it in the same mutation if needed: only a giver the store is visible to has loadout emoji, so no such kudos is lost to a late activation. Its hint reads the member's loadout: the first emoji in it and the workspace's kudos emoji, or, with every slot empty, the Inventory tab. Each step's activation is stored per member, once, with its time, its promised reward and the catalogue version, and the mutation that stores it publishes journeyStepActivated with the step and that time, so a step the activation finishes at once is published active before questFinished: `first-thanks` by the first mutation that sees the member while the slice applies, the kudosGiven subscriber for the giver or App Home's publish for the viewer, and each other step by Quests' unlockOpened subscriber for its feature, at the opening's time, which then republishes App Home, and App Home's publish activates a step the subscriber hasn't, such as one whose feature was opened while the slice didn't apply, at the same opening time, whichever runs first, for an opening after the ship moment only, since the join owns those at or before it, except `two-days` for a Book opened by the moment that the join left inactive because the Book didn't apply then, which App Home's publish, whatever tab it shows, activates once the Book applies again, at that publish, so no kudos from while the Book didn't apply counts and the activation reads no history; an opening by Unlocks' launch activates nothing, since steps for members already playing are P91's. The Journey ships in a workspace at the first mutation that sees the slice apply there in the workspace decision, which is after Unlocks launched: the kudosGiven, unlockOpened and questFinished subscribers, App Home's publish, the commands or the hourly sweep. That mutation stores the ship moment once, with its time, and schedules the join: a page of 100 members per mutation, so no mutation schedules more than Convex allows, each member who existed before the moment joined in a mutation of its own. The join activates, at the moment, every step whose feature the member had opened by then and finishes, with its reward as usual, each step that is already true then: `first-thanks` when one of the member's awards up to the moment carries a stored gate decision that says why, read from their effects records as giver in one index range, so an award recorded without a decision doesn't count and the member's next kudos that says why finishes it, `someone-new` when every current member besides them was thanked, and each later step with what followed the moment but was read before the join ran. An opening from before the moment that reaches the unlockOpened subscriber after it is left to the join, so its step counts from the moment too. It sends the member at most one DM, naming every step it finished with an Open button each, and republishes App Home when it finished one. The others count from the moment (P91). Turning a flag off and on again never moves the moment or runs the join again, and members who join later follow the schedule. A member the slice applies to while their workspace has no ship moment is previewing quests: the first mutation that sees the slice apply to them stores their preview moment once, with its time, and in that same mutation joins them alone as of that moment, as the ship moment's join joins a member who existed then, and never stores a ship moment; until the workspace's ship moment, their steps count from their preview moment. The workspace's join later treats a previewer like every member who existed then, step by step: a step they already have keeps its activation and pays nothing again, a step whose feature they opened since without it becoming active is activated at the ship moment, and its DM names only the steps that join finished (F22, F23). It subscribes to kudosGiven with one subscriber, which counts the weekly quests and then follows the Journey in one mutation, and finishes `first-thanks` when the giver's award carries a stored gate decision that says why (P12), read from the event and never from the text: it has progression record the step's reward record with exactly the promised reward and that kudos message as its cause, and publishes questFinished, keyed by member and step, with the reward's XP and coins, and has feedback send the member one DM naming the step and what it earned with an Open button, only when that record was new. The other steps count only what follows their activation (P90) and finish the same way: `first-quest` on a questFinished for a weekly quest finished at or after it became active, and in the kudosGiven subscriber itself when that kudos finished a weekly quest, so the questFinished that follows finds it recorded, and `someone-new` on a kudosGiven at or after it became active with an award to someone the giver had never thanked before that award, in the order awards were recorded, and `two-days`, by its own rule and never another step's, on a kudosGiven at or after it became active whose stored gate decision says why, while it applies, once 2 different giver's days say why, counting this one: the day and time of the first such kudos, stored on the activation by the subscriber that counted it, so a finish is dated by the later of its two kudos whichever subscriber runs first, and the days of the giver's awards made after the opening but before the activation was stored, up to this kudos, whose kudos messages' stored gate decisions say why, read where the engine stored them before spending and never from effects records, so the order progression and Quests handle a kudosGiven in doesn't matter, no kudos made while it didn't apply after its activation was stored ever counts, and no subscriber reads the giver's whole history; A weekly quest a kudosGiven finishes, and each step it finishes at that kudos' own instant, records that kudos message as its cause; a `two-days` finish dated by the other, later kudos, a finish by the join, an activation, a recheck or the questFinished subscriber records none. For the activity it answers the quests one kudos message finished for its giver from the reward records with that cause, each with its goal and reward XP. Activation also finishes a step with what followed the opening but was read before the activation ran: the first weekly quest finished, the first award to someone the member had never thanked, or the award that says why on a second different day, at or after the opening. `someone-new` also finishes whenever every current member besides the member has been thanked by them, checked when it becomes active, on each memberLeft the directory publishes, for every member of the workspace whose step is unfinished, a page of 100 members per mutation, each checked in a mutation of its own, and on each memberRejoined for the member who came back, so a small or shrinking workspace never strands it. Every finish of a weekly quest or Journey step, and only the mutation that writes its reward record, reads the member decision `crate-rewards` with exposure, beside the `quests` flag's module, only where questsApplyTo holds for the member, so a Journey join queued before Quests turned off for them reads and exposes nothing and pays at the finish, and, while it is on, has progression record the reward held (CR1, CR15): the held record counts for nothing until its crate is opened, so the finish adds no XP, level, skill point or coin and publishes no xpRecorded or levelReached, and republishes App Home itself so the crate shows even where no DM is sent; everything else the finish does stays, including `first-quest` finished by a held weekly quest, which is held too (CR2); nothing else reads the flag, and a record once held waits for its open whatever the flag says later, while a record written without `held`, before the flag or while it is off, counted at its finish and stays counted (CR4, CR5). A finished step's crate waits for the member for good: the openQuestCrate command opens the clicking member's own crate, named by its quest and never by a member from the payload, and only where a reward record is stored for it; it stores one crate opening (member, reward key, time) beside the reward record, never on it, and, for a held reward, the crate opening also stores what the open applied, `applied: { xp, coins, from, to }`, with `from` and `to` the member's `{ xp, level, coins, skillPoints }` read just before and just after in the same mutation, as progression applies the held reward's XP and coins and publishes xpRecorded and one levelReached for the highest level crossed (CR3, CR8), while a reward that counted at its finish gets no `applied`, and publishes questOpened, naming for a weekly quest the pool ID of the quest in its slot, the swapped-in one where the week has a swap, and a second open, a replayed click or a stale button writes nothing (P85, P92, P93). Quests ships with the quests flag in Unlocks' schedule, so it becomes ready at level 2 only where the quests flag is on, and is shown only where unlocks is. The quest pool is versioned catalogue data: each quest has a stable ID, its kind (breadth, thoughtfulness or participation), its goal copy, its progress predicate (what it counts of the member's given recognitions, at most 3 in a week), its needs and its reward of 20 XP and 10 coins from pool version 2 (CO6), and it holds only quests whose needs have shipped, at least one of which needs nothing but the member and stays feasible on the last day of a week with one teammate. Quests subscribes to unlockOpened for the Quests feature and draws the member's quests for the week of the opening, once per member and week; an opening by Unlocks' launch draws nothing there, so the launch schedules nothing per member, and the hourly sweep, App Home's publish or the member's next kudos draws that week instead: the member's week starts Monday 00:00 in their time zone, or in UTC without one, and is keyed by that Monday. The draw ranks the pool by a hash of member, week and quest and takes the first 3 that are feasible, checked against stored facts at the draw: people left to meet a goal (every current member besides the member for anyone, those the member never thanked, those not thanked in the 30 days before) days left in the week, counting the draw's day only while the member has kudos left that day, and recognitions left that week, at most one per person and day within each day's kudos (P16); the facts are read when the draw runs, which is also its time; with fewer feasible, it draws fewer, and with none, such as before any teammate is in the directory, it stores nothing, so a later draw that week can still give 3. It stores the draw with its week, its time and each slot's quest, reward, catalogue version and the facts that made it feasible, publishes questsDrawn naming the member, the week, the drawn quests by their pool IDs and the draw's time in the same mutation, for every draw however it was made, and republishes App Home. From then on, a member who opened Quests gets a new draw for each of their weeks, by the same rule but from the facts at the week's start (the whole of its first day's kudos, every day of the week, and whom the member had thanked before that instant), stored with the week's start as its time whenever it runs, and no DM: an hourly sweep pages through the members, 100 per mutation, and schedules one draw for each member who opened Quests, has none for their current week and whom the slice applies to, plus one mutation for the next page, so no mutation schedules more than Convex allows; and a mutation that needs the current week's draw and finds none draws it first: the kudosGiven subscriber for the giver, before it counts the award and at the award's time, never before the opening, so the award that drew the week counts, App Home's publish for the viewer, so a member who opens App Home at 00:05 on Monday sees that week's quests, and the rerollQuest command before it looks for the week to swap in. Each new draw republishes App Home. Once per member's week, the rerollQuest command swaps one unfinished quest of the clicking member's current week, named by its week and slot and never by a member, for the first quest of the same ranking that isn't in that week's draw and is feasible against the facts read at the swap, which is also its time (P81): it stores one swap per member and week, never changed and never undone, with the replaced quest and the new quest, its reward, catalogue version and the facts that made it feasible, and publishes questSwapped naming the member, the week, the replaced and the new quest by their pool IDs and the swap's time; the slot keeps its key (P87), and the new quest counts only awards made at or after the swap. A second swap that week, a swap of a finished quest, of another week or with no feasible quest left outside the draw stores no swap and publishes nothing. The same kudosGiven subscriber counts the weekly quests: an award counts toward a slot of the giver's draw for the week of the award's day only when it was made at or after the draw's time, which for every week after the one Quests was opened in is the week's start (the week's start is the boundary, so last week's unfinished quests end with it, with no penalty and no carry-over, while a finished quest's crate stays openable after its week), and only where the slot's quest counts it, decided by the pure quest progress function from the stored gate decision read from the event (P12) and from when the giver last thanked the recipient before that award, in the order awards were recorded; each counted award is stored once per member, week, slot and award with the slot's quest and never changed, so a redelivered kudosGiven, an edit or a retry never changes progress already counted (P84). A slot's progress is what its quest counts of those awards (qualifying recognitions, one per person and day (P16), different people or different days) up to its target. When it reaches the target, the quest finishes: progression records the reward record keyed by member, week and slot with exactly the reward and catalogue version stored at the draw, or at the swap for a swapped-in quest, Quests publishes questFinished naming the week, the slot, the quest's pool ID and the reward's XP and coins, and feedback sends the member one DM naming the goal and what it earned with an Open button, only when that record was new; a finished slot counts nothing more. A draw exists only once the member opened Quests, so nothing counts or finishes before (P76). After progress that changes what App Home shows, Quests republishes it. Last, the kudosGiven subscriber has feedback release the giver's success reply held for it (P71), naming each weekly quest and then each Journey step whose reward record that mutation wrote, each with its own reward, so a quest is named only in the reply of the kudos whose award finished it, and where the slice doesn't apply it releases the reply unchanged; a redelivered kudosGiven finds no held reply and releases nothing. It gives App Home the current week's quests once Quests is opened, each with its goal, its progress toward its target and its reward, and a Swap button while a swap is available (none stored this week and a feasible quest left outside the draw), or done with an Open button until its crate is opened, the oldest 5 finished, unopened weekly crates of earlier weeks, each with its goal, its reward and an Open button, and how many more wait behind them, so App Home stays within Slack's 100 blocks and opening one brings the next, and the Journey's activated steps, active with their goal and reward, done with an Open button until its crate is opened, or opened, and the website questsOf: the current week's key, draw time, its swap with the swap's time, whether a swap is available, and each slot's quest, goal, promised reward, feasibility facts, progress, target, finish time and crate opening, and each activated step's activation time, promised reward, catalogue version, finish time and crate opening. The slice applies to a member only where the quests flag (off by default) and xp-levels are on in their member decision and unlocks applies to them, on in their decision and already launched by Unlocks in their workspace or opened by their unlocks preview moment, which Quests never starts itself; every mutation and query about one member reads that one predicate, so a member who isn't previewing draws, earns and opens nothing because another member previews, and where it doesn't apply Quests records and shows nothing for that member: no draw, counted award, swap, reward record, Journey ship moment, activation or crate opening, no DM, no held or named success reply, and no Journey, Quests section, crate or Swap on App Home, and Quests is never ready or next there. A stale Open or Swap clicked there writes and publishes nothing, and turning a flag off and on again rewrites nothing already recorded and shows it all again unchanged. The join belongs to its ship moment: a join mutation that runs after the slice stopped applying still joins as of the moment, and its DM is checked at delivery like every quest DM. A success reply's quest line is checked again when Slack takes the reply, so where the slice stopped applying meanwhile the reply goes out without it. Quests sends the quests exposure once per member and value, from App Home's publish for the viewer, and for the giver from the kudos' own mutation, which decides whether the success reply waits for Quests, and from its kudosGiven subscriber, and only where xp-levels is on and unlocks applies to that member, since only there does the quests flag decide anything, each exposure carrying the member's own decision (O36, F25). Ending a preview hides quests from the member as turning the flag off does, and keeps every draw, reward, finish and crate opening (F24). For the website, Quests keeps each Journey step's goal and hint copy, the copy App Home shows, offers a query-safe projection of the Journey that lists first-thanks active with the catalogue's current reward while no activation is stored, writing nothing, and reads every finished, unopened weekly crate of an earlier week with the week it was drawn in, the goal of the quest it finished and its recorded reward, oldest first. That read, for App Home and the website alike, reads only the pending-crate index: one row (member, reward key) per finished weekly crate not opened yet, written in the mutation that records the quest's reward and removed in the mutation that stores its crate opening, so its cost follows the crates still waiting rather than every weekly reward the member ever earned; each deploy runs the index's backfill as its last step, whenever the backend deployed, even after a website step failed, an action that pages through the reward records, 100 per mutation, until the last page before it returns, so a failed page fails the deploy, and reconciles each weekly one: a row for one with neither a crate opening nor a row, and no row for one with an opening, so crates finished before the index wait again once it returns; it stores its cursor and when it finished, so a failed run resumes where it stopped and, once finished, a later run reads no reward record and writes nothing, except that the shared sandbox's deploy restarts it each time, since a lane may have deployed code from before the index there in between.
