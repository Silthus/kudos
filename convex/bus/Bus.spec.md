# Bus

Event bus: publishes domain events to the registered subscribers in the publisher's transaction: kudosGiven to the welcome message, progression, Quests' Journey, the leaderboard's totals, and the analytics module's recording of kudos given and kudos received; attemptFailed and memberWelcomed to the analytics module's recording of attempt failed and member welcomed; levelReached, which progression publishes, to Unlocks, which records the level up and its readiness and sends the level-up DM in one mutation, and to feedback's public level notice; xpRecorded, which progression publishes from the transaction that records a member's XP, to feedback's App Home refresh; unlockReady and unlockOpened, which Unlocks publishes and nothing subscribes to yet; and questFinished, which Quests publishes and nothing subscribes to yet. levelReached carries the kudos message only when an award reached the level; a level reached through a reward record has none, and the public level notice posts nothing for it. Commands are not domain events: the command registry in convex/commands routes them.
