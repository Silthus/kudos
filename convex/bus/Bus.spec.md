# Bus

Event bus: publishes domain events to the registered subscribers in the publisher's transaction: kudosGiven to the welcome message, progression, Quests' Journey and weekly quests, Achievements' ladder counts, the leaderboard's totals, the album's grant of thoughtful packs, scheduled for every kudos message (HH7), and the analytics module's recording of kudos given and kudos received; attemptFailed and memberWelcomed to the analytics module's recording of attempt failed and member welcomed; memberGreeted, which the welcome module publishes from a Monday-path welcome, feedback from a welcome nudge it sends, and the website's sign-in callback and the Slack adapter's verification from a member's first sign-in, each saying whether the greeting owes the welcome item, to Items' welcome item; levelReached, which progression publishes, to Unlocks, which records the level up and its readiness and sends the level-up DM in one mutation, to feedback's public level notice, and to the analytics module's recording of level reached; xpRecorded, which progression publishes from the transaction that records a member's XP, to feedback's App Home refresh; unlockReady, which Unlocks publishes, to the analytics module's recording of unlock made ready; unlockOpened, which Unlocks publishes, to the analytics module's recording of unlock opened, scheduled only for an opening that is not the launch's, to Quests' weekly draw, scheduled only for the Quests feature, to the Journey's activation, scheduled only for a feature that opens a Journey step and never for an opening by Unlocks' launch, and to feedback's Coins opening DM, scheduled only for Coins and never for an opening by Unlocks' launch, since a subscriber may name which events it takes so that Unlocks' launch, which opens every shipped feature for every member already playing in one mutation, schedules nothing for the others; unlocksLaunched, which Unlocks' launch publishes for every opening it stored, 1,000 openings to an event, to the analytics module's recording of unlock opened, so the launch schedules one analytics job per 1,000 openings rather than one per opening, and no job's arguments come near Convex's 8,192 elements in an array; questFinished, which Quests publishes with the reward's XP and coins and, for a weekly quest, its pool ID, to the Journey's `first-quest`, scheduled only for a weekly quest, to the welcome module's first-thanks welcome, scheduled only for the Journey's `first-thanks`, and to the analytics module's recording of quest finished, and questOpened, which Quests publishes with a weekly quest's pool ID, to the analytics module's recording of crate opened; questsDrawn and questSwapped, which Quests publishes when it stores a weekly draw and a swap, to the analytics module's recording of quest drawn and quest swapped; journeyStepActivated, which Quests publishes where it stores a Journey step's activation, to the analytics module's recording of journey step activated; achievementEarned, which Achievements publishes when a member earns a tier and nothing subscribes to yet; perkBought, which progression publishes from the mutation that buys a perk and nothing subscribes to yet; hoggieDropped, published from the mutation of a crate's first open that drops a hoggie, to the analytics module's recording of hoggie dropped; stickersCollected, which Stickers' live subscriber publishes from the mutation that records new stickers, listing each with its collector, person, first award and that award's giver, to the analytics module's recording of person collected; thoughtfulPacksGranted, which the album publishes once per thoughtful kudos message from the page that finds every recipient granted, with each recipient's outcome, to the analytics module's recording of packs earned; stickerPutIn, which the album publishes once per album sticker a member puts in from their tray, naming the collector and the person, to the analytics module's recording of album sticker collected; itemTurned, published once per item from the mutation of its first turn, with its source, the drawn rarity and whether it is new, to the analytics module's recording of item turned; companyTeamsReplaced, which the company profiles sync publishes from the mutation that replaces the company copy, to the album's reservation of company team emoji names; itemBought, which the store publishes from the mutation that buys an item, with its price, and nothing subscribes to yet; memberLeft and memberRejoined, which the directory publishes when a member stops being one and when one comes back, to the Journey's `someone-new`, and memberLeft also to the engine, which forgets the words the member wrote; and primaryGroupChanged, which Groups publishes with each change of a member's primary group, its cause and instant, and nothing subscribes to yet. levelReached carries the kudos message only when an award reached the level; a level reached through a reward record has none, so its public level notice goes only to the announcement channel, keyed by the member and the level, and nowhere while none is picked. Each publish gives its event a fact ID of its own, the same in every subscriber's delivery, so a subscriber can tell the same fact delivered again from another fact with the same values. Commands are not domain events: the command registry in convex/commands routes them.
