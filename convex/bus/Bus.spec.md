# Bus

Event bus: publishes domain events to the registered subscribers in the publisher's transaction: kudosGiven to the welcome message, progression, Quests' Journey and weekly quests, Achievements' ladder counts, the leaderboard's totals, and the analytics module's recording of kudos given and kudos received; attemptFailed and memberWelcomed to the analytics module's recording of attempt failed and member welcomed; levelReached, which progression publishes, to Unlocks, which records the level up and its readiness and sends the level-up DM in one mutation, to feedback's public level notice, and to the analytics module's recording of level reached; xpRecorded, which progression publishes from the transaction that records a member's XP, to feedback's App Home refresh; unlockReady, which Unlocks publishes, to the analytics module's recording of unlock made ready; unlockOpened, which Unlocks publishes, to the analytics module's recording of unlock opened, scheduled only for an opening that is not the launch's, to Quests' weekly draw, scheduled only for the Quests feature, and to the Journey's activation, scheduled only for a feature that opens a Journey step and never for an opening by Unlocks' launch, since a subscriber may name which events it takes so that Unlocks' launch, which opens every shipped feature for every member already playing in one mutation, schedules nothing for the others; unlocksLaunched, which Unlocks' launch publishes for every opening it stored, 1,000 openings to an event, to the analytics module's recording of unlock opened, so the launch schedules one analytics job per 1,000 openings rather than one per opening, and no job's arguments come near Convex's 8,192 elements in an array; questFinished, which Quests publishes with the reward's XP and coins and, for a weekly quest, its pool ID, to the Journey's `first-quest`, scheduled only for a weekly quest, and to the analytics module's recording of quest finished, and questOpened, which Quests publishes with a weekly quest's pool ID, to the analytics module's recording of crate opened; questsDrawn and questSwapped, which Quests publishes when it stores a weekly draw and a swap, to the analytics module's recording of quest drawn and quest swapped; journeyStepActivated, which Quests publishes where it stores a Journey step's activation, to the analytics module's recording of journey step activated; achievementEarned, which Achievements publishes when a member earns a tier and nothing subscribes to yet; perkBought, which progression publishes from the mutation that buys a perk and nothing subscribes to yet; and memberLeft and memberRejoined, which the directory publishes when a member stops being one and when one comes back, to the Journey's `someone-new`. levelReached carries the kudos message only when an award reached the level; a level reached through a reward record has none, so its public level notice goes only to the announcement channel, keyed by the member and the level, and nowhere while none is picked. Commands are not domain events: the command registry in convex/commands routes them.
