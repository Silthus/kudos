# Activity

Activity: a member's own activity, read for the website module through one seam, activityOf for a page of the member's kudos received and given, filtered to all, received or given, and recentKudosOf for the last few of one direction. It reads awards, the gate decision progression recorded, the kudos text, the directory, the workspace's URL and its user groups through their owners' exported reads, never their tables. Each received award is one entry and each message the member gave is one entry with every recipient, ordered newest first by instant, then given above received, then the newer document first, so two entries never compare equal; a page reads each kind's index from the cursor's position down, at most one more entry than its size, and the cursor and every entry's key encode only a position, never an ID. Each kudos entry names other members by their display name or "A teammate" with an initials face, says whether the kudos said why from the decision recorded when it was given, carries its channel's visibility from the kudos text while its words are readable, private otherwise, its Slack permalink once the workspace's URL is known, and its words with person and group mentions resolved as the kudos text's own read answers them, else why they are unavailable. It answers the member's time zone beside each page. What each given kudos earned is null until progression's share is read.
