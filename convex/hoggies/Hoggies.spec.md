# Hoggies

Hoggies: the hoggies and team crests members collect from the crates they open (CL1–CL26, decision 25), knowing nothing about why a crate was opened. It owns the versioned hoggie catalogue, version 1 being every hoggie in the pinned `@posthog/brand` 0.13.0 with one rarity each, common, uncommon, rare, epic or legendary, weighted 50, 28, 14, 6 and 2, and version 2 adding the package's 54 team crests, each with a frozen roster of two to four version 1 hoggies, every hoggie on exactly one; the draw, a pure function of the deployment's `HOGGIE_DRAW_SECRET` (the empty string where it is unset), the member, the crate key and the catalogue version, through SHA-256, whose first 4 bytes pick the rarity by cumulative weight and whose next 4 pick the hoggie in catalogue order; the `collection` flag, a member decision off by default, read without exposure everywhere but the drop; the `crests` flag, a member decision off by default, read without exposure everywhere but an open that completes or answers a crest; the pack bands (CV3), versioned catalogue data, version 1 giving every crate kind 5 to 25 XP in steps of 5 and 1 to 5 coins; the pack draw (CV4), a pure function of the same secret, the member, the crate key and the band version, through SHA-256 of `{secret}:{member}:{crate}:pack:{version}`, whose first 4 bytes pick the XP step and next 4 the coins, each uniformly within the band and independently of the hoggie; the `packs` flag, a member decision off by default, read without exposure everywhere but the drop; and each member's insert-only drop records, unique per member and crate key, with the hoggie, its rarity at the draw, the member's drop sequence `n`, the hoggie's `copy`, the catalogue version, the open's time, from version 2 the crest the drop completed or null, and the pack bonus its open paid with the member's standing with coins before and after it, beside a tally per member and hoggie and one per member, written in the drop's mutation so no read counts the history. Its one write, `dropFor`, called by Quests and Unlocks in the mutation of a crate's first successful open of a weekly quest, a group quest, a Journey step or a feature (CL6), after the open's own writes and before the open's event, reads `collection` with exposure, draws, then reads `packs` with exposure and, while it is on, pays the crate's pack bonus through `applyGain` (CV5), stores the drop and its tallies with the crest it completed whatever `crests` says and the bonus it paid, and publishes hoggieDropped with the bonus's XP and coins, or does nothing where the crate already dropped or `collection` is off; `packOf` answers the bonus a crate's drop paid, with its standings, read from the drop record; `dropOf` answers a crate's stored drop and `dropsNewestFirst` a member's drops by time and then `n`, both read from the drop records. `collectionOf` answers every catalogue hoggie with the member's count and first find, each rarity's chance, held and catalogue counts and, while `crests` is shown, every crest with its roster, the roster hoggies held and when it was collected, at the first find of the roster hoggie with the highest drop sequence, never stored as its own record, read from the member's tallies through one index range of at most one row per catalogue hoggie, never the drop records; `hoggiesOf` answers App Home how many distinct catalogue entries the member holds, the catalogue's size, their newest drop and, while `crests` is shown, how many crests they collected, read from those tallies and the one newest drop record. It also sells crates for coins (CL27–CL31): `buyCrate`, reached through Commands, answers null and writes nothing while `collection` or the `crate-store` flag (a member decision off by default, read with exposure only there) is off for the member; it answers a crate number the member already bought as bought again and writes nothing, so a double click or a retried request buys once, refuses any other number but the member's bought crates so far plus one as stale, then refuses while coins aren't visible to the member, then spends the catalogue's price of 50 coins through the wallet's spend step for `{ crate }` (refusing no-coins, writing nothing), and stores the bought crate `bought:{n}` with its number and time and publishes crateBought, all in one mutation; `openCrate` opens only a bought crate of the member's that isn't opened yet, only while `collection` is on, drawing its drop as `dropFor` does and marking the crate opened, so a crate opened while `collection` is off keeps waiting, while turning `crate-store` off stops only buying. `waitingCratesOf` answers the member's unopened bought crates and `crateStoreOf` the price and the next number while coins are visible. It also keeps each member's avatar (#537): `setAvatar`, reached through Commands, does nothing while `collection` or the `hoggie-avatar` flag (a member decision off by default, read with exposure only there) is off for the member, and otherwise stores the hoggie as the member's one avatar, or removes it for none, only where the member holds a tally of that hoggie, refusing any other and changing nothing, so a repeat leaves the same state; `avatarShownOn` answers a viewer a member's avatar, the hoggie with its catalogue name, rarity and art, or null, only while both flags are on for the viewer and for the member, read without an exposure, and nothing otherwise, and `avatarChoiceOf` answers App Home the member's avatar and every hoggie they hold, in catalogue order. Only this module writes drop records, tallies, bought crates and avatars.
