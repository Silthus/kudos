# Bus subscriber replay audit

Audited source: [`df092e346d11f55fc5e86977715a8a03445820ce`](https://github.com/Silthus/kudos/tree/df092e346d11f55fc5e86977715a8a03445820ce), the research worktree's starting HEAD. Date: 2026-10-29. This is a throwaway research branch, never a merge candidate. No production query, event replay, deployment or product-code change was made. All probes use synthetic local fixtures.

Checked against fetched `main` at `0c67b447b799f2549d088ba5e83ac29222fbf9fa`: its only backend changes since the baseline are welcome copy and its assertions. The registry and the replay protections audited here are unchanged.

## Finding

**An arbitrary repeat delivery is unsafe for the analytics subscribers, quest-judgment markers, legacy first-hug reply, coins-opening DM, grant reply and message-linked level celebration.** The weekly-draw handler also changes its target when replayed in a later week. The existing recognition payments, pack/item earning and board increments already have durable keys; a second delivery does not double-pay them under the audited paths.

The complete registry inventory is below. “Keyed” means the handler checks a durable fact or entitlement key before its business effect, within the same mutation. “Convergent” means it recalculates or replaces a current-state view. These labels do not promise zero redundant schedules or identical results after flags, directory state, collections or catalogues change.

## What a failed-job replay actually guarantees

The [current publisher](../../convex/bus/publish.ts) schedules handlers directly and has neither an event receipt nor `bus.replay`. Every registered handler in this snapshot is an `internalMutation`, even though the registry's type also permits actions. The [proposed architecture](https://github.com/Silthus/kudos/blob/c62dea538a4503ae2c86a6ce38f5a9d81a7e2e29/docs/architecture/hugs-hook.md) describes the future replay API; it is not implemented in this snapshot.

[Convex's scheduling contract](https://docs.convex.dev/scheduling/scheduled-functions#scheduling-from-mutations) makes scheduled writes atomic with their mutation. A failed mutation commits neither its database writes nor its scheduled child effects. Retrying exactly that failed mutation is different from delivering an already-committed fact again. Convex retries internal transient errors; developer errors fail the job. Scheduled job results are available for seven days, so the proposed system-table replay only works while its source job remains available.

**Do not call the original producer or republish a whole event to repair a failed subscriber or child.** Successful siblings may have committed, and keyed parents often return without recreating an already-scheduled child. Replay the actual failed function with its original arguments. Also atomically record the original failed job's replacement when scheduling it: its original `failed` state does not change, so repeated operator calls would otherwise create parallel replacements. This replay receipt complements the subscriber keys; it does not make successful-fact redelivery safe. Exclude side-effecting actions unless their external delivery protocol is separately proven.

For these mutation handlers, a single correctly targeted repair has no partially committed effect to duplicate. The defects below therefore block arbitrary duplicate fact delivery; they do not by themselves block a failed-mutation-only replay with an atomic replacement claim. Keep these two guarantees distinct in the implementation and its tests.

## Repairs, with the proof each owes

### Analytics: deduplicate the fact before projecting into the outbox

Every registered analytics handler shares [outbox `record`](../../convex/analytics/outbox.ts), which inserts `crypto.randomUUID()` for every product event. The [drain](../../convex/analytics/drain.ts) retains that UUID while retrying one outbox row, but invoking the subscriber again creates a distinct row and UUID. This is duplicate analytics, not duplicate XP or wallet credit.

The smallest shared repair is an analytics receipt keyed by subscriber and immutable fact identity, inserted in the same mutation as its whole projected outbox batch. Keep it after successful drain deletes the rows. Existing row UUIDs already cover drain retries; they need no replacement if the durable receipt prevents a second batch. `kudosGiven` can use the full message reference; hug facts can use giver plus full message reference. Some current events cannot identify every logical occurrence safely: `itemTurned` has no item key, and repeated failed attempts may have the same message and values. Add an optional fact identity to the scheduling envelope or carry the missing source key; do not deduplicate everything by timestamp, member, a newly generated replay UUID or a hash of an incomplete payload. Preserve compatibility with already-queued `{ event }` jobs, assigning a replay identity from the original failed job where needed.

Proof: deliver every analytics subscriber twice, including projections with multiple recipients or participants, drain/delete its outbox rows, deliver again, and observe one product event per logical projection. Also show that distinct turns or attempts with matching visible values remain distinct.

### Quest judgments: make the marker itself idempotent

[Quest progress](../../convex/quests/weekly.ts), [journey finishes](../../convex/quests/journey.ts) and [rewards](../../convex/quests/finish.ts) are keyed. [`judgeQuestsOf`](../../convex/quests/judged.ts) is not: it blindly inserts markers, while readers use `.first()`. Repetition grows duplicate rows even with quests switched off, without double-paying a quest. Query `by_award` before inserting, in the existing transaction. Do not return early from the whole subscriber solely because markers exist: a held reply may still need releasing.

Proof: repeated kudos delivery preserves the marker set, quest progress, reward/item records and a single released reply. While items still hold a combined reply, repeated quest judgment must preserve its quest lines.

### Welcome, coins and grant replies: persist the notification decision

The legacy branch of [`welcomeOnHug`](../../convex/welcome/welcome.ts) checks `first: true`, then schedules the same [first-hug action](../../convex/slack/firstHugReply.ts) each time. A durable first-reply receipt is missing. The Monday-path welcome, ordinary welcomes and nudges already have member receipts; preserve those.

[`tellCoinsOpened`](../../convex/feedback/coins.ts) has no notice key. [`notify`](../../convex/feedback/notify.ts) schedules a new action on every call; the [delivery action](../../convex/slack/directMessages.ts) does not deduplicate separate jobs. Add the coins-opening receipt in the scheduling mutation. This repeats a DM, not a coin payment.

[`replyAfterTheGrant`](../../convex/feedback/albumReplies.ts) has no fallback receipt. Worse, [`tellItemsInTheReply`](../../convex/feedback/replies.ts) returns false after the first call clears `awaitsItems`, so even a grant already absorbed into the combined reply later falls through to another album reply. Record the disposition under message/giver as either absorbed or fallback scheduled, before returning. A genuinely late grant after the reply timeout must still take the fallback path once.

Proof: legacy and Monday welcomes, coins openings, legacy grant replies, grant-before-quests, quests-before-grant and timeout-before-grant each delivered twice cause the intended visible message once. A pre-action receipt prevents duplicate scheduling; it does not prove exactly-once external delivery after a timeout. Keep Slack's uncertain-send/rate-limit handling separate from bus replay, and do not replay send actions by default.

### Public level sections: preserve monotonic state

[`setPublicSection`](../../convex/feedback/publicReplies.ts) replaces `level:{user}` and bumps the version unconditionally. A duplicate on a posted row schedules an unnecessary edit; replaying an earlier level after a later one replaces the later text. The reward-only announcement path already has a member/level receipt. Store the highest applied message/member level, skip an identical or lower fact, and schedule only a changed section. A generic equality check alone fixes extra edits but does not stop an old level replacing a newer one.

Proof: replay before posting, after posting and after a newer level under the same kudos. Keep the newer level and perform no extra edit for an unchanged section.

### Opening-triggered draw: distinguish event time from catch-up time

[`drawWeeklyQuests`](../../convex/quests/weekly.ts) calls `drawFor(..., Date.now(), ...)`. The member/week receipt protects an immediate duplicate, but a later-week replay targets a different receipt. Prefer `event.at` for the opening's effect, with the existing `drawNewWeek` responsible for present-week catch-up. This is a research recommendation for the implementing lane, not a product rule change.

Proof: repeat an old opening in a later week without another draw or another `questsDrawn`; the ordinary weekly sweep still draws the current week.

## Continuations and limits of the replay allowlist

Self-paging handlers are already registry members and keep their additional arguments: `from`, `cursor`, Achievements' carried `untold`, and attribution's `unreconciled`. Rebuilding only `{ event }` starts them over and can lose carried notification state.

Separate internal workers are not covered merely by matching the registry. Explicitly decide and test these if their failures must be recoverable:

| Parent subscriber | Separate worker | Existing protection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `album/tallies:tallyHugGiven` | `album/tallies:tallyHug` with `hug` and `after` | Hug/person tally receipts. |
| `quests/journey:recheckSomeoneNew`, `recheckRejoined` | `quests/journey:recheckSomeoneNewOf` | Quest finish/reward key. |
| `album/milestones:recordMilestonesAfterTheMatches` | `album/milestones:recordMilestonesOfStickerHolders` with workspace/after | Milestone-tier and item keys. |
| `engine/kudosText:forgetWordsOfLeaver` | `engine/kudosText:forgetAuthoredWords` | Deletion request and tombstone transition. This is a destructive current-state scrub; inspect rejoin/new-text scope before a late replay. |
| `stickers/stickers:recordStickers` | `stickers/completions:checkTeamPages`, `stickers/backfill:backfillStickers` | Completed team set, tier rewards and missing-sticker checks; backfill uses its stored cursor. |

The [failure sweep](../../convex/analytics/sweep.ts) reports failed jobs; it does not repair them. A successful parent with a failed delivery action is a delivery repair, not permission to replay its committed bus fact. Record a repair path per worker rather than widening the allowlist to all internal functions.
