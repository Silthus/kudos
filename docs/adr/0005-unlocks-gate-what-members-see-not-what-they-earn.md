# Unlocks gate what members see, not what they earn

Features open one level at a time: quests at 2, the leaderboard at 3, the Book at 4, the skill tree at 5, coins and the inventory at 6, hedgehogs at 7. Reaching the level makes a feature ready, and the member opens it. An unlock changes only what a member is shown and can act on. Everything they earn, from XP and coins to stickers, achievement tiers and quest rewards, is recorded from their first kudos whatever they have opened, and waits for them in the feature when they open it. We chose this over earning only after opening, because effects are stored once with their award ([ADR-0004](0004-effects-are-stored-with-their-award.md)) and must not depend on when someone clicked a button, and because a member who opens late should find more, not less. Features that had shipped before unlocks stay open for the members who had them ([ADR-0002](0002-progression-is-permanent.md)), and a feature that hasn't shipped is never ready or named.
