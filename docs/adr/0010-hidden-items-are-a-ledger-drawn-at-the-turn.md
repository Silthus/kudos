# Hidden items are a ledger, drawn at the turn

Every reward a member has not seen (a thanked person's sticker, a pack's hoggie, a level-up's three hoggies, the welcome hoggie) is one row in an `items` ledger, written in the same mutation that earns it and marked turned once. An item is a sticker or a hoggie, nothing else. The item board and the Slack reply's count each read one index range of it. A hidden hoggie gets no drop record until its turn, so "face down" is the absence of a record rather than a filter every collection, activity and milestone reader has to apply, and a pack's 15 XP pays at that turn, as it pays at the open today.

We chose a stored ledger over an item board derived on each read from the packs, the tray and the level-ups. Deriving it would read all three on every read, the Slack reply's included, and still need a record for level-up hoggies that have not been drawn. We chose drawing at the turn over drawing at the earn and hiding the result: hiding would put a "not turned yet" check in front of every reader of drops. Items are earned whatever the `hidden-items` flag says, as packs are ([ADR-0006](0006-unlocks-gate-what-members-see-not-what-they-earn.md), [ADR-0009](0009-the-album-is-collection-first.md)), and the old reveals (the put-in and the level-up drop) mark theirs turned, so the flag can go on or off at any time. A backfill earns what waited before the ledger existed, tray contents included, and nothing stored is deleted.

Earned emojis are never items and never hidden: each unlocks with the turn that brings it, after the item lands. The turn records the milestone tiers it reaches in the same mutation and answers them, so milestones stay inline rather than following the bus. The turn never rolls a random emoji; the sandbox's old open keeps its roll until the tray retires. These are the product owner's calls in the core-loop spec (LP3, LP13, LP14, LP15 in `spec/core-loop.md`), which amend the architecture's emoji source, its random roll at the turn and its move of milestones to the bus. The spec wins where the two differ.
