# Two currencies with different jobs

Progression has two currencies. **Skill points** come one per level and buy perks: they say what kind of player you are. **Coins** come from thoughtful giving and from being thanked by many different people, and buy what you save up for: perk upgrades, cosmetics, streak freezes and XP boosters, and later real-world rewards an admin sets up. Coins never turn into kudos and kudos never turn into coins. We chose this over skill points alone or a single currency because two currencies give the economy both choices and saving goals. The balance research found that selling XP boosters lets coins speed up skill points, and through capacity perks, indirectly the kudos a member can give; the product owner accepted that route in exchange for boosters and a store that can grow beyond the game.

Settled in grilling [#9](https://github.com/Silthus/kudos/issues/9). Coherence journal decision: `d-9ee35336`.

**Amended on [#86](https://github.com/Silthus/kudos/issues/86) (2026-10-13), after the scope cut (map #1, decision 12):** coins now buy perk upgrades, kudos emoji and hedgehogs; XP boosters, streak freezes and cosmetics by rarity are gone. Coins also come from quests and from bronze and diamond achievement tiers. Coherence journal decision: `d-01e321cd`.
