# The album is collection-first

HedgeHug's game becomes an album of the people you thanked. A thoughtful kudos earns its giver one thoughtful pack for each person it thanks, at most one per giver, person and week. The first pack for someone holds their sticker, and every pack holds a hoggie. People join a member's album only through packs they earned by giving, plus the people they gave a kudos award to before the album began. Being thanked never adds anyone. Each real small team from posthog.com is a page, and earned emojis mark a complete team, a milestone's gold, levels 5 to 25 and a member's first thoughtful pack for a person. Levels become status and unlock nothing. Coins, the store, the crate store, weekly and group quests, the Journey, the skill tree and the old ladders retire by turning their existing flags off, and nothing stored is deleted.

We chose this over today's game of coins, a store, quests, a Journey, a skill tree and seven achievement ladders. Nothing in that game rewarded the one thing the product exists for: thanking a colleague and saying why. We also chose it over collecting people in both directions, as the Book's stickers do. A collection that fills when others thank you asks nothing of you.

Packs are recorded from the first deploy, whatever the `album` flag says, as earnings are ([ADR-0006](0006-unlocks-gate-what-members-see-not-what-they-earn.md)). A pack is never taken back by an edit or a deletion ([ADR-0001](0001-awards-are-permanent.md)). Each earned emoji's name is stored once in a per-workspace registry and never moves, because a member adds it to Slack by hand under that name.
