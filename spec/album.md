## Problem Statement

HedgeHug today is a game with too many parts. A member who opens it meets coins, a store, a crate store, weekly quests, group quests, a Journey, a skill tree, levels that unlock features, seven achievement ladders and a Book with five chapters. The research on #629 and #630 found that most thank-yous in Slack give no reason, that thoughtful shout-outs are rare, and that the five-word rule barely tells kudos apart. Nothing in the game rewards the one thing the product exists for: thanking a colleague and saying why. Members collect people by being thanked as much as by thanking, so the collection doesn't ask anything of them.

The product owner redrew the destination on 2026-10-18 (Q1–Q15 on the map) and accepted a calm album as the website (#625, round 6): "people are the collection; hoggies are the surprise". He then asked for the whole thing to be built and shipped to production: "Now lock it in and tell the conductor to build the whole thing and ship it to prod with full autonomy."

## Solution

**A thoughtful kudos earns a pack. The pack puts the person you thanked into your album and rolls a hoggie. The website is that album.**

- **The loop.** Thank someone and say why, and you earn a pack for them, at most one per person per week. The first pack for a person holds their sticker; every pack holds a hoggie. You open packs on the website, the opened stickers and hoggies wait in a tray, and you put them in. People reach your album only through packs you earned by giving, plus the people you thanked before the album existed.
- **Teams.** Each real small team from posthog.com is a page in your album. Collect everyone on it, then click to unlock its team sticker, which earns its team emoji.
- **Earned emojis.** A team emoji per team sticker, a milestone emoji at each tier of each milestone (#707), a level emoji at levels 5, 10, 15, 20 and 25, and a hype emoji per person for your first thoughtful kudos to them. HedgeHug always shows the emoji's picture. Slack can only gain a custom emoji by a person's hand outside Enterprise Grid, so HedgeHug offers each one to add and says "use it in Slack" only once Slack has it (AL28–AL32).
- **Calm.** Hugs stay quiet: 1 XP, no message, a Monday recap. Levels are status and unlock nothing. Coins, the store, the crate store, weekly and group quests, the Journey, the skill tree and the old ladders go dark behind flags, and nothing stored is deleted.
- **The website** is the Team yearbook: `/` is your profile, your team's Class photo one click away, a top bar with the XP bar, packs, the tray, the emoji popup, your profile and settings, Activity and the Leaderboard as icon buttons, and a sidebar of teams, `Hoggies` and `Milestones`. Every URL Slack sends today keeps landing.
- **Slack** gets three moments: a thread reply after a thoughtful kudos, the Monday hug recap, and a weekly post of the top thoughtful kudos. Each names the emojis it unlocked. The app stays reactive.
- **Dark first, widened one flag at a time**, each judged before and after on the activation dashboard (MS1–MS6), then the old shell is deleted.

## Rules

"`album` is on for a member" means the flag answers true in their member decision. "Thoughtful" means the giver's gate decision on the kudos message says thoughtful (`convex/progression/gate.ts` today: five or more words; the `thoughtful-ai` classifier stays dark). "The giver's week" is the Monday-start week `Progression` reckons for the giver (P17). Every new flag is a boolean declared through Analytics' flag seam with safe default `false` and is created in PostHog project 662840 at `environment = sandbox`, 100%, by the ticket that declares it, never by this spec.

### Stopping point 1: the core loop in the backend

#### Packs

- **AL1. A thoughtful kudos earns a pack.** For every recipient of a thoughtful kudos message, the giver earns one **thoughtful pack** for that recipient in the giver's week, unless they already earned one for that recipient that week. A message to several recipients earns one pack per recipient. Hugs, non-thoughtful kudos and a giver thanking themselves earn none.
- **AL2. One per giver, person and week, exactly.** A thoughtful pack is unique on (giver, recipient, the giver's week). A redelivered `kudosGiven`, two messages in one week, two subscribers racing and a message edited afterwards never make a second. An edit or a deletion of the kudos message never takes a pack back (awards are permanent, ADR-0001).
- **AL3. Earned whatever the flags say.** Thoughtful packs are recorded from the first deploy, whatever `album` says, as stickers were (SB6) and as earnings are (ADR-0006's principle). `album` decides only what members are shown and can act on. A member the flag turns on for later finds the packs they earned.
- **AL4. Out of the giving transaction.** Packs are granted by a new `kudosGiven` subscriber of the `Album` module, in scheduled mutations of at most 50 recipients each, each scheduling the next, never in the giving mutation, which is near Convex's range limit. One recipient costs at most four index ranges: the pack's uniqueness, the giver's album sticker of the recipient, the giver's earlier pack that already holds the recipient's sticker, and the message's grant record for that recipient. Each page writes one insert-only **grant record** per recipient `{ giver, message, recipient, outcome }`, where `outcome` is `new` (the pack holds their sticker), `held` (they're in the album already), `waiting` (an earlier pack or the tray already holds their sticker) or `nextWeek` (no pack: one was earned this week).
- **AL5. What a pack holds.** A thoughtful pack holds the recipient's **sticker** when, at its grant, the giver holds no album sticker of them and no earlier pack of theirs holds it; otherwise it holds no sticker. Every thoughtful pack holds one hoggie, drawn when it is opened (AL8). The pack records at its grant whether it holds the sticker, so the answer never changes with the order packs are opened.
- **AL6. One completed fact per message.** The page that writes a message's last grant record publishes `thoughtfulPacksGranted` once, `{ giver, message, outcomes }`, guarded by a unique per-message marker, so a redelivery or a retried page never publishes twice. Every consumer of a message's packs reads this fact, never the pack table at its own time, since the bus schedules subscribers independently (`convex/bus/publish.ts`). Analytics projects it as a new product event `packs earned` with `packs_earned` (the count of `new`, `held` and `waiting` outcomes) and `recipients`, the giver as subject and no person named. This replaces MS13's reservation of `packs_earned` on `kudos given`, which Analytics' kudos subscriber could compute before the grant ran. `hoggie dropped` gains the crate kind `thoughtful` (MS13). Analytics only observes (E11).
- **AL7. A pack has a crate key.** A thoughtful pack's crate key is `thoughtful:<the pack's stored ID>`, never a Slack ID, so `dropFor`'s deduplication and draw work unchanged.

#### Opening, the tray and putting in

- **AL8. Opening.** A member opens one of their own unopened thoughtful packs with a website command. In one mutation the open draws the hoggie through `Hoggies`' `dropFor` with crate kind `thoughtful` and `surface: website` (MS12), pays the pack bonus where `packs` is on, records the open, and moves the pack's sticker, if it holds one, and its hoggie into the member's **tray**. A second open of the same pack, a pack of someone else and an open while `album` is off change nothing and answer a fixed refusal.
- **AL9. The pack bonus.** A thoughtful pack pays 15 XP and no coins, through the existing pack bonus (CV3, CV7) as band version 2's entry for the kind `thoughtful`. Coins never come from a thoughtful pack.
- **AL10. The tray is stored.** The tray is the member's opened stickers and hoggies not yet put in, in opening order. It is read from the stored opens, so a reload or another device shows the same tray.
- **AL11. Putting in.** A member puts one tray item in, or all of them, with a website command. Putting a sticker in records an **album sticker** `{ member, person, from: "pack", pack, at }`; putting a hoggie in marks it put in. Putting an item in twice, or an item not in the member's tray, changes nothing. The tray's **put-in order** is a pure function of the stored items: hoggies first in opening order, then stickers grouped by team page (the member's team first, then the sidebar's order), each page's completing sticker last. `putAllIn` puts in at most 25 items in that order per call and answers the items it placed, in order, plus `more`; the website calls it again until `more` is false, so "Put them all in" is one action for the member however long the tray is. Each call recomputes the order over the tray as it is then, so an item opened on another device during the sequence takes its place in the next call's order. A retried page places nothing twice.
- **AL12. A hoggie in the tray is already found.** `Hoggies` records the drop at the open, as today; the `Hoggies` page and every count read a hoggie from a thoughtful pack as found only once it is put in. `Hoggies` from older crates are found as they were.

- **AL73. The album's hoggies.** `getAlbumHoggies` answers the member's hoggie collection for the album, owned by `Album` and read from `Hoggies`' exported collection functions, independent of the Book, `achievements` and `unlocks`: every hoggie of the catalogue version the album shows, with its rarity, found or still hidden, its count and spares, its first-found date, the rarity counts found and total, and the rarest finds; hoggies from thoughtful packs count only once put in (AL12).

#### The album's stickers

- **AL13. Collected only by giving.** A member's **album stickers** are the people they put in from thoughtful packs (AL11) and the people they gave a kudos award to before the album's history was taken (AL14). Being thanked never adds anyone. Today's Book stickers, recorded in both directions (SB1–SB6), stay stored and keep being recorded for the old shell until it is deleted; the album never reads them.
- **AL14. History.** A one-off backfill, `startAlbumHistory`, pages one workspace's awards in award order, 50 per mutation, and records an album sticker `{ from: "history", at: the earliest award }` for every (giver, recipient) pair with an award decided before the backfill began. Its start instant is stored once; awards after it collect only through packs. Run twice, or racing a pack, it records each pair once (unique on member and person). Its pages then fill the pair tallies (AL46) and week tallies (AL48) from the same awards and from the workspace's hugs. It records no product event, as the sticker backfill doesn't (MS10).
- **AL15. Your own face.** A member's own sticker is always in their own album. It needs no record and is never in a pack.
- **AL16. `Album` stickers are permanent.** An album sticker is insert-only and kept when the person leaves the workspace or posthog.com.
- **AL17. The analytics event.** Putting a sticker in publishes `stickerPutIn`, which Analytics projects as `album sticker collected` with the collector as subject and no property naming the person. History records nothing. `person collected` (MS10) keeps meaning today's Book stickers and is retired from the dashboard's Collecting row when the old shell is deleted.

#### Team pages and team stickers

- **AL18. Team pages are company teams.** A member's team pages are the company teams Company Profiles keeps from posthog.com, each with its matched people other than the member, held where the member holds their album sticker, and its unmatched people shown but not counted, as team pages count today (CV30), read through the matcher every reader uses. A page is complete when it counts at least one person and holds all of them. A team with more than six people shows them all; nobody is dropped.
- **AL19. Unlocking a team sticker.** On a complete page, a website command `unlockTeamSticker(team)` records a **team sticker** `{ member, team, at, name, art, emoji }` once, with the team's slug as `team`, its name and crest art as the company copy holds them, and its emoji name from the registry (AL25). On an incomplete page, an unknown team, a second click and while `album` is off it changes nothing and answers a fixed refusal. The page answers `ready` (complete, not unlocked), `unlocked` with its date, or its progress.
- **AL20. A team sticker stays unlocked.** A team sticker stores, at its unlock, the team's slug, name, crest art and emoji name, so someone joining the team, a lost match, the team leaving posthog.com or a renamed team (which posthog.com shows as a new slug, so a new team) never takes it back or changes how it shows; its page reads unlocked with its date from the stored copy when the team is gone from the company copy.

#### `Milestones` and levels

- **AL21. Five milestones.** The album has five **milestones**, each with bronze, silver, gold and diamond steps, counted from album facts and recorded insert-only per tier with its date the first time a count reaches it:
  - *Faces in the album*, album stickers held: 1, 10, 25, 50.
  - *Full pages*, team stickers unlocked: 1, 5, 15, and a diamond `D`.
  - *Across the company*, distinct company teams with at least one album sticker: 3, 10, 25, and a diamond `D`.
  - *Hoggie hunter*, distinct hoggies found: 10, 40, 100, and every hoggie in the catalogue version the album shows.
  - *Kind words*, thoughtful kudos awards given: 1, 25, 100, 500.

  `D` is fixed in the milestone catalogue when it is built: the largest multiple of 5 not above the number of company teams with at least two matched members on that day. The catalogue is versioned; a later change of the real team count changes no recorded tier. Each milestone's emblem is today's ladder art (`connections`, `team-spirit`, `old-friends`, `recognition`, `generosity`), and each tier has a name, as in the prototype. Every tier earns its own milestone emoji (AL23, #707).
- **AL22. Levels are status.** XP and levels stay as `Progression` computes them (L2 at 20 XP, then `50·(L−1)·(L−2)`). In the album a level unlocks nothing; it shows as a status line with an XP bar. Levels 5, 10, 15, 20 and 25 earn level emojis (AL23).

### Stopping point 2: earned emojis

- **AL23. Four kinds, all earned.** An **earned emoji** is one of:
  - a **team emoji**, earned by unlocking that team's sticker (AL19), named `:team-<slug of the team's name>:`, for example `:team-workflows:`, so a pack of the crests installs in Slack at once (#735);
  - a **milestone emoji** per milestone tier, earned by reaching that tier (AL21), named `:<slug of the tier's name>:`, so bronze, silver, gold and diamond across the five milestones earn 20; a member who held a tier before it earned an emoji holds its emoji (#707, replacing gold only);
  - a **level emoji**, earned by reaching level 5, 10, 15, 20 or 25, named `:level-<n>:`; a jump past several earns each;
  - a **hype emoji**, earned by the member's first thoughtful pack for a person (AL1), named `:<slug of the person's full name>-hype:`. History stickers earn none: thanking them thoughtfully once more earns it.

  Nothing else earns an emoji at random: a **random emoji**, a Slack custom emoji the maintainer classes as a random drop (AL74), drops now and then from an opened thoughtful pack.
- **AL24. Derived entitlement, stored names.** Whether a member has an earned emoji is derived on every read from what earns it: their team stickers, milestone tiers (each tier its own), level and thoughtful packs, so it never drifts from its cause. Only two things are stored: the names (AL25) and what was seen (AL26).
- **AL25. One namespace, names that never move by themselves.** Every earned emoji's name lives in one per-workspace **emoji name registry** `{ emoji, name }`, unique on both, and never reassigned when people join, leave or are renamed; only the maintainer's mapping (AL74) moves a name, apart from the move of an unmapped team name not under `team-` to `team-<slug>` (#735), and the emoji's ID, which is what a member holds, never changes. Shared names are reserved before anyone earns them: level names and every milestone tier's name when the registry is first used, and in a registry opened before a catalogue name existed, the names it lacks at the next reservation (after each company sync, or by hand) (#707); the gold tier keeps the registry key it had while gold alone earned a milestone emoji, so no name given then moves, and each company team's name the first time the company copy holds the team (a job after each sync). A hype name is allocated at its first grant. So a shared name is never lost to a hype name that came first, and the shared list (AL32) can show every shared name before its first unlock. A name is the slug of its source (lower-cased, accents stripped, every run outside `a–z` and `0–9` turned into one hyphen, trimmed, cut to 80 characters, `-hype` appended for hype emojis and `team-` prepended for team emojis, #735); a slug that is empty becomes `team-<n>` or `person-<n>-hype` by registry order; a slug already in the registry, or one of Slack's standard emoji names (a vendored list in the catalogue), gets the first free `-2`, `-3`, … A name already taken by someone else's custom emoji in Slack is kept and shown as AL30 says. In the reservation after each company sync, every team emoji no one mapped whose name isn't under `team-` (a bare slug from before this rule, or one older code wrote after a rollback) moves to its `team-<slug>` name, even where Slack already has that name, which is then its own emoji in Slack; a team the maintainer or a member mapped keeps its name, a legacy name already under `team-` stays, and no row is added or removed. Since every team name given from then on is under `team-`, a team renamed later keeps its name (#735).
- **AL26. Seen.** Each earned emoji appears once in a moment. A website command `markEmojisSeen(ids)` records the member's seen emojis, insert-only; an emoji earned and not seen is one the next album visit shows. A member who already earned emojis before the album sees them on their first visit. Several waiting emojis come as one moment, not a queue: a bundle that opens like a pack and shows up to 5 at once, each with its picture, its Slack name and what it came from; past 5 it says "+N more", which opens the emoji popup to see them. Taking the bundle marks exactly the emojis it showed seen; "+N more" marks every waiting emoji seen, since the popup lists them (#721).
- **AL27. Art.** Team emojis use the team's crest art from the catalogue (`@posthog/brand` PNG exports), milestone emojis each tier's medal, its emblem in the tier's metal (#707), level emojis a level emblem drawn from the brand. #### How earned emojis reach Slack (the delivery decision)

- **AL28. Slack can't be written to.** Slack's only API that creates a custom emoji, `admin.emoji.add`, needs an Enterprise org, an org-wide install and an admin's user token. The app is installed on one workspace (`org_deploy_enabled: false`) with a bot token, so HedgeHug never creates, renames or removes a Slack emoji, and requests no new scope.
- **AL29. HedgeHug always shows the picture.** The website and every Slack moment show an earned emoji as its picture: an `<img>` on the website, a `Block Kit` `image` element in a `context` block in Slack, from a public URL (AL27). Slack never receives a bare `:name:` for an earned emoji that Slack doesn't have, since Slack would print the name as text. The ticket that first posts one proves Slack accepts the blocks (`ok: true`, and `conversations.replies` returns the image elements) in the sandbox workspace.
- **AL30. "In Slack" is read, not assumed.** An earned emoji is **in Slack** while the workspace's custom emoji copy (`Workspace`'s `customEmoji`, kept by `emoji.list` on `emoji_changed` and daily) holds its name. Only then does the website say "use it in Slack: react with it or put it in a kudos", and only then may a Slack moment write `:name:` beside the picture. A name Slack already has is that Slack emoji, mapped by name (AL74): it counts as in Slack, and the album pictures it as Slack does.
- **AL31. Members find it in Slack; adding it is the last step** (#719, the product owner, 2026-10-19: "allow users to do the mapping in an easy popup mini wizard and the admin, but don't offer a download and prompt them to add. only if the emoji is not found").
